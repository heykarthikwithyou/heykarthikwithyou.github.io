Seiko 5 Sports SRPK65K1 'Checker Flag' Special Edition Review (2026) – Stylish Automatic Watch with Petrol Blue Dial

Features

  • Seiko 5 Sports SRPK65K1 Special Edition
  • Petrol Blue 'Checker Flag' Dial
  • Automatic Mechanical Movement
  • Stainless Steel Case
  • Stainless Steel Bracelet
  • Day and Date Display
  • Hardlex Crystal Glass
  • Exhibition Case Back
  • Unidirectional Rotating Bezel
  • LumiBrite Hands and Markers
  • Water Resistant Design
  • Fold-Over Clasp with Safety Lock

Pros

  • Unique special edition design with an eye-catching petrol blue dial
  • Reliable Seiko automatic movement requires no battery
  • Premium stainless steel construction offers excellent durability
  • LumiBrite hands and markers improve visibility in low light
  • Exhibition case back showcases the automatic movement
  • Comfortable bracelet suitable for everyday wear
  • Versatile design works well for both casual and smart-casual occasions
  • Excellent craftsmanship and long-term reliability

Cons

  • Automatic movement requires regular wear or manual winding
  • Hardlex crystal is less scratch-resistant than sapphire crystal
  • Slightly heavier than watches with leather or silicone straps
  • Bracelet may need resizing for a perfect fit
  • Premium pricing compared to standard automatic watches

Verdict

The Seiko 5 Sports SRPK65K1 'Checker Flag' Special Edition is an excellent choice for watch enthusiasts who appreciate mechanical craftsmanship and distinctive design. Its petrol blue dial and sporty styling make it stand out, while the automatic movement delivers the reliability and engineering that Seiko is known for.

The stainless steel case and bracelet provide a premium feel, and thoughtful features like the LumiBrite hands, day-date display, exhibition case back, and rotating bezel enhance both functionality and style. Whether worn daily or reserved for special occasions, this watch offers a refined balance of performance and aesthetics.

If you're looking for a premium automatic watch with collector appeal, dependable mechanical movement, and timeless styling, the Seiko 5 Sports SRPK65K1 is an outstanding investment.
