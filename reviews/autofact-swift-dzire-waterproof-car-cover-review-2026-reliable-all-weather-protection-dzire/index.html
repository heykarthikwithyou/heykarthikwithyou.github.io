  • Compatible with Maruti Swift Dzire (2017–2024) – Tailored fit designed specifically for Dzire models.
  • Waterproof 4x4 American Matty Material – Helps protect the car from rain, dust, bird droppings, and sunlight.
  • Triple-Stitched Construction – Improves durability and long-term usage strength.
  • Mirror & Antenna Pockets – Better fitting design for easier installation and cleaner appearance.
  • Full Body Coverage – Covers the entire car body for enhanced outdoor protection.
  • Elastic Bottom Fit – Helps keep the cover secure during windy conditions.
  • UV & Heat Protection Support – Helps reduce paint fading and interior heat buildup.
  • Lightweight Yet Durable Material – Easier to handle during daily usage.
  • Scratch & Dust Protection – Helps reduce direct exposure to dust and environmental particles.
  • Suitable for Indoor & Outdoor Parking – Useful for apartment parking and open outdoor areas. 

Pros

  • Good protection against rain and dust
  • Tailor-made fit for Swift Dzire models
  • Triple stitching improves durability
  • Mirror and antenna pockets improve fitting accuracy
  • Helps reduce direct sunlight exposure
  • Useful for outdoor apartment parking
  • Elastic fit helps during windy weather
  • Better than basic thin generic car covers

Cons

  • Waterproof covers can trap dust if used on dirty cars
  • Material may feel heavy during daily removal and installation
  • Long-term outdoor usage may reduce fabric life over time
  • Strong winds can still affect fit if not secured properly
  • May cause minor micro-scratches if dust gets trapped underneath 

Verdict

The Autofact Swift Dzire Waterproof Car Cover is a practical option for Indian Dzire owners looking for reliable everyday protection against dust, sunlight, rain, and outdoor exposure.

Its biggest strengths are the tailored Dzire fit, durable triple-stitched construction, and waterproof material that provide better protection compared to generic low-cost car covers. Features like mirror pockets, antenna support, and elastic bottom fitting also improve usability during regular daily use.

For users who park their car outdoors regularly or want to reduce paint exposure to harsh weather conditions, this cover offers good overall value. However, like most waterproof car covers, it works best when used on a clean car to avoid dust-related paint scratches over time.

Best for:

  • Outdoor parking protection
  • Apartment and roadside parking
  • Daily dust and rain protection
  • Swift Dzire owners wanting tailored-fit covers

Skip if:

  • You want ultra-lightweight quick-use covers
  • You frequently cover dusty cars without cleaning
  • You need premium soft inner-lining protection
