Verdict

The OPPO Enco Air 5 is an impressive premium true wireless earbud that delivers an excellent balance of sound quality, active noise cancellation, battery life, and everyday usability. Its adaptive 52dB real-time ANC effectively blocks surrounding noise, making it suitable for commuting, office use, flights, and focused listening sessions.

Audio performance is another strong point, with large dynamic drivers producing punchy bass, clear vocals, and detailed highs. Combined with Hi-Resolution Audio support and 3D Spatial Audio, the earbuds provide an immersive experience for music, movies, and gaming. The triple-microphone AI noise cancellation system also ensures clear voice calls, even in challenging environments.

Battery life is among the best in its segment, offering up to 54 hours of total playback with the charging case, while fast charging quickly gets you back to listening. Features like Bluetooth 5.4, low-latency gaming mode, IP55 protection, and companion app customization make the overall package highly competitive.

If you're looking for premium TWS earbuds with powerful noise cancellation, excellent battery life, clear calling performance, and rich sound quality without spending flagship-level money, the OPPO Enco Air 5 is one of the best choices available in 2026.
