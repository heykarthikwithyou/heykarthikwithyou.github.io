Verdict

The De'Longhi Dedica Style EC685.W is one of the best entry-level premium espresso machines for home users who want authentic Italian-style coffee without investing in a large commercial setup. Its 15-bar pressure pump and Thermoblock heating system consistently produce rich espresso with excellent crema, while the manual steam wand gives you full control over milk texturing for lattes, cappuccinos, flat whites, and other café favorites.

Its slim 15cm-wide design makes it ideal for compact kitchens, yet it doesn't compromise on performance. The machine works with both freshly ground coffee and E.S.E. pods, making it suitable for beginners as well as users looking to improve their home barista skills. Many coffee enthusiasts also appreciate that the EC685 can be upgraded later with accessories such as a bottomless portafilter and a quality grinder for even better espresso extraction.

If you're looking for a stylish, reliable, and compact espresso machine capable of making café-quality espresso, cappuccinos, lattes, and iced coffee at home, the De'Longhi Dedica Style EC685.W remains one of the strongest choices in its segment.
