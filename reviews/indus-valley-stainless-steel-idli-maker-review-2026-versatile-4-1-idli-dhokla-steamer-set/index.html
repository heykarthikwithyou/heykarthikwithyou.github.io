Verdict

The Indus Valley Stainless Steel Idli Maker is more than just an idli cooker—it's a complete steaming solution for modern kitchens. With dedicated plates for idlis, dhoklas, and momos, along with a general-purpose steamer plate, it offers excellent versatility for preparing a variety of healthy dishes.

Its food-grade stainless steel body and 3-layer thick base provide durability and even heat distribution, while compatibility with both induction and gas stoves makes it suitable for almost any kitchen setup. The generous 4.2-litre capacity also makes it practical for medium to large families.

If you're looking for a premium stainless steel steamer that can handle multiple recipes while promoting healthier cooking with minimal oil, The Indus Valley Multi Kadai and Steamer Set is a reliable and worthwhile addition to your kitchen.
