Features

  • Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 Processor – Flagship-level performance for gaming, multitasking, and heavy apps 
  • 11.2-inch 3.2K Display (144Hz) – Smooth scrolling, sharp visuals, ideal for OTT and reading 
  • Ultra Slim Metal Design (≈5.7mm) – Premium feel, lightweight, easy to carry anywhere 
  • 9200mAh Battery – Easily lasts a full day with mixed usage 
  • HyperOS with AI Features – Smart productivity tools, multitasking, and ecosystem integration
  • Quad Speakers with Dolby Atmos – Great for movies and YouTube binge sessions
  • Wi-Fi 7 Support – Future-ready connectivity for faster internet speeds

Pros

  • Flagship-level performance at mid-range price
  • Excellent display for movies, gaming, and reading
  • Premium metal build (feels like expensive tablets)
  • Long battery life – perfect for travel and work
  • Great value compared to iPads in India
  • Smooth UI with good multitasking support

Cons

  • No cellular (SIM) support – Wi-Fi only
  • Camera quality is average (not for serious photography)
  • Accessories like keyboard/stylus cost extra
  • Not a full laptop replacement for heavy professional work

Verdict

The Xiaomi Pad 8 is one of the best value-for-money Android tablets in India right now.

You’re getting flagship performance, premium design, and a top-class display at a price where most brands offer only mid-range specs. It’s perfect for students, professionals, and casual users who want a fast, reliable, and future-ready tablet.

👉 Best for:

  • Students (notes, classes, PDFs)
  • Content consumption (Netflix, YouTube, OTT)
  • Light productivity & multitasking
  • Gaming (BGMI, COD Mobile, etc.)

👉 Skip if:

  • You need SIM calling/data
  • You want a full laptop replacement
  • You prefer iPad ecosystem
