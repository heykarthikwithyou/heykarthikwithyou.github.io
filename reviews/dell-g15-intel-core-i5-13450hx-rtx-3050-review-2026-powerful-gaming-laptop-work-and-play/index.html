Dell G15 (Intel Core i5-13450HX & RTX 3050) Review (2026) – Powerful Gaming Laptop for Work and Play

A performance-focused gaming laptop featuring a 13th Gen Intel Core i5 processor, RTX 3050 graphics, and a fast 1TB SSD for gaming, content creation, and multitasking.

Features

  • 13th Gen Intel Core i5-13450HX Processor
  • NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 6GB Graphics
  • 16GB DDR5 RAM
  • 1TB PCIe NVMe SSD
  • 15.6-inch Full HD Display
  • Windows 11 Home
  • Microsoft Office Home 2024 Included
  • Orange Backlit Keyboard
  • Advanced Cooling System
  • Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth Connectivity
  • Multiple USB Ports, HDMI & Ethernet
  • Nahimic Audio Technology
  • Dark Shadow Grey Finish
  • 2.65kg Build

Pros

  • Powerful HX-series processor delivers excellent gaming and multitasking performance
  • RTX 3050 6GB handles modern games and creative applications smoothly
  • 16GB RAM ensures seamless multitasking
  • 1TB SSD offers fast boot times and ample storage
  • Efficient cooling system supports long gaming sessions
  • Backlit keyboard enhances usability in low-light environments
  • Sturdy build quality suitable for long-term use
  • Suitable for gaming, programming, video editing, and 3D workloads

Cons

  • Heavier than most standard laptops at 2.65kg
  • Battery life is limited during gaming
  • Display may not match premium models in color accuracy
  • Cooling fans become noticeable under heavy loads
  • RTX 3050 is less future-proof than newer RTX 40 or 50 series GPUs

Verdict

The Dell G15 is an excellent mid-range gaming laptop that offers a strong balance of performance, durability, and everyday usability. The Intel Core i5-13450HX processor paired with the NVIDIA RTX 3050 provides enough power for modern AAA games, competitive esports titles, software development, video editing, and other demanding workloads.

Its 16GB RAM and 1TB SSD ensure smooth multitasking and plenty of storage for games, applications, and media. Dell's cooling system helps maintain stable performance during extended gaming sessions, while the backlit keyboard adds convenience for both gaming and productivity.

If you're looking for a reliable gaming laptop that also performs well for work, programming, and creative tasks without reaching flagship pricing, the Dell G15 is a solid investment.
