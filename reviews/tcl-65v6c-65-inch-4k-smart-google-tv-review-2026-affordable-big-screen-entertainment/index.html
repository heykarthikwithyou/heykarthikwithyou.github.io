Verdict

The TCL 65V6C is a strong value-for-money 65-inch smart TV for users who want a large-screen entertainment experience without spending premium-brand prices. Its 4K Ultra HD panel, HDR10 support, and AiPQ processor produce sharp visuals with vibrant colors, while MEMC helps deliver smoother motion during sports and action scenes.

Google TV remains one of the biggest highlights, offering a fast and intuitive interface with thousands of streaming apps, built-in Chromecast, Google Assistant, and personalized content recommendations. Dolby Atmos support, multiple connectivity options, and the modern bezel-less design further enhance its appeal for everyday entertainment.

While the native 60Hz panel and average brightness mean it isn't designed for high-end gaming or premium HDR performance, it performs very well for movies, OTT streaming, cable TV, and casual gaming.

If you're looking for an affordable 65-inch Google TV with solid picture quality, modern smart features, and excellent overall value, the TCL 65V6C is one of the best choices in its price segment.
