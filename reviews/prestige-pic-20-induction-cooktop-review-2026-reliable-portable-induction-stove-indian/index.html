Features

  • 1600W Heating Power – Supports faster cooking and efficient energy usage for daily meals.
  • 8 Preset Indian Menu Options – Includes dedicated cooking modes for common Indian dishes and preparation styles.
  • 4KV Surge Protection – Added electrical safety protection for Indian power conditions.
  • Soft Touch Button Controls – Easy and responsive operation with modern touch interface.
  • Built-In Timer Function – Helps improve cooking convenience and precision.
  • Portable Compact Design – Easy to move and suitable for small kitchens, hostels, and travel usage.
  • Energy Efficient Cooking – Reduces heat wastage compared to traditional gas stoves.
  • Easy-to-Clean Surface – Flat ceramic-style top simplifies maintenance and cleaning.
  • Automatic Voltage Regulation – Helps maintain stable performance during voltage fluctuations.
  • BIS Certified Safety Standards – Reliable safety-focused appliance design.
  • Lightweight Construction – Convenient for temporary setups and everyday portability.
  • 1-Year Warranty – Added after-sales support from Prestige.

Pros

  • Fast heating improves cooking speed
  • Preset Indian menus are useful for beginners
  • Portable design suits small kitchens and hostel users
  • Easy cleaning compared to gas stoves
  • Surge protection improves safety
  • Energy-efficient cooking performance
  • Soft-touch controls feel modern and convenient
  • Good value-for-money induction cooktop

Cons

  • Requires induction-compatible cookware
  • Fan noise is noticeable during operation
  • Heating area may feel small for larger utensils
  • Touch controls may need careful handling with wet hands
  • Not ideal for very large family cooking requirements

Verdict

The Prestige PIC 20 Induction Cooktop is a practical and reliable option for Indian households looking for fast, portable, and energy-efficient cooking.

Its biggest strengths are the preset Indian cooking menus, compact portability, and electrical safety features that make it highly suitable for students, bachelors, small families, and backup cooking setups. The induction heating also reduces kitchen heat and offers quicker cooking compared to traditional gas stoves.

While it requires compatible cookware and may not fully replace large multi-burner gas setups for bigger families, for daily convenience cooking and portable usage, it offers excellent overall value.

Best for:

  • Small families and bachelors
  • Hostel and PG users
  • Backup kitchen cooking
  • Fast everyday cooking
  • Portable cooking setups

Skip if:

  • You frequently cook using oversized utensils
  • You need multi-burner cooking flexibility
  • You do not have induction-compatible cookware
