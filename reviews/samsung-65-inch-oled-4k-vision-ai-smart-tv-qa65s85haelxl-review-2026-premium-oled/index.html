Verdict

The Samsung QA65S85HAELXL is an excellent premium OLED TV that delivers exceptional picture quality for movies, gaming, and everyday entertainment. Its self-illuminating OLED panel produces perfect blacks, rich contrast, and vibrant colors, while the NQ4 AI Gen2 Processor intelligently enhances both picture and sound to provide a premium viewing experience.

For gamers, the TV offers a native 120Hz refresh rate, Variable Refresh Rate (VRR), Motion Xcelerator 120Hz, and HDMI 2.1 gaming features that ensure responsive gameplay with minimal motion blur. Movie enthusiasts will also appreciate OLED HDR, Filmmaker Mode, Dolby Atmos support, and the anti-reflection display that maintains excellent visibility even in well-lit rooms.

If you're looking for a premium 65-inch OLED TV with cutting-edge AI features, stunning picture quality, and excellent gaming capabilities, the Samsung Vision AI OLED S85H is one of the best choices in its price segment for 2026.
