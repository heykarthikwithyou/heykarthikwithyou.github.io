Acer Aspire Lite (Intel Core i5-12450H) Review (2026) – Powerful Thin & Light Laptop for Productivity and Multitasking

Featuring a Full HD IPS display, backlit keyboard, and lightweight design, it's built for students, professionals, programmers, and multitaskers who need reliable everyday performance.

Features

  • 12th Gen Intel Core i5-12450H Processor
  • Intel UHD Integrated Graphics
  • 16GB DDR4 RAM
  • 512GB PCIe NVMe SSD
  • 15.6-inch Full HD IPS Display
  • Anti-Glare Screen
  • Windows 11 Home
  • Microsoft Office Home 2024 Included
  • Microsoft 365 Basic (1-Year)
  • Backlit Keyboard
  • Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth Connectivity
  • Multiple USB Ports, HDMI & Audio Jack
  • 1.7kg Thin and Lightweight Design
  • Pure Silver Finish

Pros

  • Powerful Intel Core i5 H-series processor delivers excellent multitasking performance
  • 16GB RAM is ideal for productivity, programming, and running multiple applications
  • IPS display offers better viewing angles and color consistency than standard TN panels
  • Fast 512GB SSD ensures quick boot times and responsive performance
  • Backlit keyboard is convenient for working in low-light environments
  • Lightweight design makes it easy to carry for work or college
  • Windows 11 and Microsoft Office Home 2024 are included
  • Excellent value for users seeking high performance without a premium price

Cons

  • Intel UHD graphics are not suitable for modern AAA gaming or intensive graphics work
  • Battery life is average during demanding workloads
  • Display color accuracy is sufficient for everyday use but not ideal for professional content creators
  • Speakers are adequate but external audio devices offer a better multimedia experience
  • Plastic chassis may not feel as premium as aluminum-bodied laptops

Verdict

The Acer Aspire Lite AL15-52H is an impressive choice for users who need desktop-like performance in a portable laptop. Its Intel Core i5-12450H processor delivers noticeably better performance than standard U-series chips, making it well-suited for multitasking, software development, office work, coding, data analysis, and light photo or video editing.

The combination of 16GB RAM, a fast SSD, and a Full HD IPS display ensures a smooth and comfortable user experience for both work and entertainment. The backlit keyboard is another welcome addition, especially for users who often work during evenings or in dimly lit environments.

While integrated Intel UHD graphics limit gaming and professional creative workloads, this laptop excels in everyday productivity and business use. For students, professionals, programmers, and anyone looking for a powerful yet affordable thin-and-light laptop, the Acer Aspire Lite offers outstanding value for money.
