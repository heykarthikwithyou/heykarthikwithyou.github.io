Features

  • 13 Place Settings Capacity That Can Accommodate Up to 70 Utensils, Making It Suitable for Regular Family Dishwashing and Larger Daily Loads.
  • Intensive Kadhai Program Designed Specifically to Handle Heavily Soiled Indian Cookware Such as Kadhais, Pots, Pans, and Other Grease- and Food-Laden Utensils.
  • Hygiene+ Function That Uses High-Temperature Cleaning to Provide Enhanced Hygiene for Dishes and Kitchenware, with the product claiming removal of up to 99.9% of germs.
  • Built-In Heater That Heats Water to Support More Effective Cleaning and Helps Tackle Grease, Oil, and Stubborn Food Residue Without Manual scrubbing.
  • Multiple Wash Programs That Provide Different cleaning options for varying levels of soiling and types of utensils, helping users choose an appropriate cycle for everyday or intensive cleaning.
  • Free-Standing Design That Allows Flexible Placement in the Kitchen Without Requiring the Dishwasher to be integrated into existing cabinetry.
  • Silver Inox Finish That Gives the Dishwasher a Modern Stainless-Steel Appearance and Allows It to Blend Easily With Contemporary Kitchen Appliances.

Pros

  • 13-place-setting capacity

  • Intensive Kadhai program

Verdict

The Bosch SMS66GI01I is a strong choice for Indian households looking to reduce the daily effort involved in washing dishes and cookware. Its 13-place-setting capacity and ability to accommodate up to 70 utensils make it suitable for regular family use, while the dedicated Intensive Kadhai program addresses one of the biggest concerns Indian buyers often have when considering a dishwasher: cleaning heavily soiled cookware.

The Hygiene+ function and built-in heater further strengthen its cleaning capabilities by using high-temperature water to tackle stubborn grease and support hygienic cleaning. Having multiple wash programs also gives users greater flexibility for everyday dishes, lightly soiled loads, and more demanding cookware.

If you're looking for a large-capacity dishwasher designed with Indian cooking in mind, the Bosch SMS66GI01I is a compelling option. Its dedicated Kadhai program, Hygiene+ function, heating system, and free-standing design make it particularly suitable for families that regularly deal with oily dishes, kadhais, pots, and pans. As with any dishwasher, users should check cookware compatibility and loading guidelines to get the best results.
