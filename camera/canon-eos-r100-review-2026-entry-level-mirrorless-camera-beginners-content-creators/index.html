Canon EOS R100 Review (2026) – Entry-Level Mirrorless Camera for Beginners & Content Creators

A beginner-friendly mirrorless camera with a 24.1MP APS-C sensor, 4K video recording, and Canon RF lens compatibility for photography and vlogging.

Features

  • 24.1MP APS-C CMOS Sensor
  • DIGIC 8 Image Processor
  • RF-S 18-45mm f/4.5-6.3 IS STM Lens Included
  • Canon RF Mount
  • 4K UHD Video Recording
  • Full HD 120fps Slow Motion
  • Dual Pixel CMOS Autofocus
  • Eye Detection Autofocus
  • Continuous Shooting up to 6.5fps
  • 2.36 Million-Dot Electronic Viewfinder

Pros

  • Excellent 24.1MP APS-C sensor delivers detailed, vibrant images
  • Compact and lightweight body is ideal for travel and everyday photography
  • Dual Pixel autofocus provides fast and accurate subject tracking
  • RF mount offers access to Canon's growing mirrorless lens ecosystem
  • 4K video recording produces sharp, high-resolution footage
  • Included RF-S 18-45mm lens is versatile for everyday shooting
  • Easy-to-use controls make it ideal for beginners
  • Built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth simplify photo and video transfers

Cons

  • Fixed LCD screen limits flexibility for selfies and vlogging
  • No in-body image stabilization (IBIS)
  • 4K recording has limitations compared to higher-end EOS R models
  • Single SD card slot
  • Battery life is moderate for extended shooting sessions

Verdict

The Canon EOS R100 is an excellent entry-level mirrorless camera for beginners, hobbyists, students, and aspiring content creators. Its 24.1MP APS-C sensor captures sharp, colorful images with impressive detail, while the DIGIC 8 processor ensures responsive performance for everyday photography.

The included RF-S 18-45mm IS STM lens is well-suited for landscapes, portraits, travel, and family photography, making it a versatile starter kit. Built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth allow quick sharing of photos and videos, while Canon's intuitive menu system makes learning photography straightforward.

Although advanced users may miss features like a fully articulating touchscreen and in-body image stabilization, the EOS R100 offers excellent image quality, dependable autofocus, and access to Canon's RF lens ecosystem at an affordable price. For anyone stepping into mirrorless photography, it's one of the best value cameras available.
