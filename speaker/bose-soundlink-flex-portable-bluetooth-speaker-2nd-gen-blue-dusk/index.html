Bose SoundLink Flex Portable Bluetooth Speaker 2nd Gen – Blue Dusk

A compact outdoor Bluetooth speaker with Hi-Fi audio, up to 12 hours of battery life, and waterproof and dustproof protection.

Features

  • Hi-Fi Audio: Designed to deliver clear, balanced sound from a compact portable speaker.
  • Portable Design: Compact construction makes it easy to carry for travel, outdoor activities and everyday listening.
  • Bluetooth Connectivity: Provides convenient wireless music streaming from compatible smartphones, tablets and other devices.
  • Up to 12 Hours Battery Life: Offers up to 12 hours of playback on a full charge, depending on usage and conditions.
  • Waterproof and Dustproof: Built to handle outdoor environments with protection against water and dust.
  • PositionIQ Technology: Automatically detects the speaker's orientation and adjusts the sound for a more consistent listening experience.
  • USB-C Charging: Uses USB-C for convenient charging and everyday connectivity.

Pros

  • Compact and highly portable
  • Good sound quality for its size
  • Up to 12 hours of battery life
  • Waterproof and dustproof construction
  • Automatic sound adjustment with PositionIQ
  • Suitable for indoor and outdoor use

Cons

  • Premium pricing compared with basic portable speakers
  • No built-in Wi-Fi connectivity
  • Bass performance may not match larger speakers

Verdict

The Bose SoundLink Flex 2nd Gen is a strong choice for buyers looking for a compact Bluetooth speaker that combines portability with quality audio. Its small size makes it convenient for travel, everyday use and outdoor listening without taking up much space.

The waterproof and dustproof construction makes the speaker particularly suitable for outdoor environments, while the up to 12-hour battery life provides enough endurance for extended listening sessions. PositionIQ also adds convenience by adapting the sound based on how the speaker is positioned.

For users who prioritise portability, dependable outdoor durability and premium audio in a compact package, the Bose SoundLink Flex 2nd Gen is an attractive option. Its higher price means budget-focused buyers may find alternatives with similar basic features, but Bose's overall audio experience and build quality make it worth considering.
