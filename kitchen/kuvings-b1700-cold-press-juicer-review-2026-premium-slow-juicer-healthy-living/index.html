Verdict

The Kuvings B1700 Cold Press Juicer is one of the most trusted premium slow juicers available for home use. Its patented JMCS technology and low-speed extraction process produce nutrient-rich juice with minimal oxidation, resulting in better taste and improved juice yield compared to conventional centrifugal juicers.

The 76mm wide feeding chute significantly reduces preparation time by allowing larger fruits and vegetables to be inserted with minimal cutting. Its quiet operation, premium construction, BPA-free materials, and easy-clean design make it well suited for families who prepare fresh juice regularly. While smoothie and sorbet strainers are optional on some variants, the juicer itself offers outstanding performance for fruits, vegetables, leafy greens, and nut milk.

If you're looking for a premium cold press juicer that delivers exceptional juice quality, durable construction, and long-term reliability, the Kuvings B1700 is one of the best investments for a healthy kitchen.
