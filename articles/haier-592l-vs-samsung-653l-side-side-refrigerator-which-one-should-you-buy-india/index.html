Haier 592L vs Samsung 653L Side-by-Side Refrigerator: Which One Should You Buy in India?

Compare capacity, smart features, convertible storage, cooling performance, energy efficiency and convenience to find the right refrigerator for your home.

Haier 592L vs Samsung 653L Side-by-Side Refrigerator: Which One Should You Buy in India?

Choosing a premium refrigerator for a large family is no longer just about capacity. Modern side-by-side refrigerators now combine convertible storage, inverter compressors, smart connectivity, AI-powered features and convenience-focused designs. Two interesting options in this segment are the Haier 592L 3-Door Side-by-Side Refrigerator and the Samsung 653L Side-by-Side Refrigerator with AI, Wi-Fi and 5-in-1 Convertible technology.

Both refrigerators are designed for large Indian families, but they take slightly different approaches. Haier focuses on its three-door design, water dispenser, convertible storage and Smart Sense AI, while Samsung offers greater capacity, 5-in-1 conversion, SmartThings connectivity and AI Energy Mode.

So, which one offers better value? Let's compare them feature by feature.

1. Capacity: Samsung Has the Clear Advantage

If refrigerator capacity is your biggest priority, Samsung wins this round.

The Samsung offers 653 litres of storage, giving it 61 litres more capacity than the Haier 592L. That difference may not sound enormous on paper, but for a large family, it can provide meaningful additional space for vegetables, fruits, beverages, frozen foods, containers and bulk grocery purchases.

The Haier 592L is already generously sized and should comfortably suit a large family. However, buyers who regularly stock groceries in large quantities may appreciate Samsung's additional capacity.

Winner: Samsung

2. Design and Organization: Haier's Three-Door Approach

Haier takes an interesting approach with its three-door configuration. The additional door can make compartment organization more convenient, particularly for households that like to separate different categories of food.

The Haier also includes a built-in water dispenser, which can be particularly useful in Indian homes where chilled drinking water is frequently needed. Instead of opening the refrigerator repeatedly, users can access water conveniently from the dispenser.

Samsung uses a more traditional side-by-side design with a clean, premium appearance, recessed handles and a minimalist exterior. Its official specifications also highlight a built-in look and internal display.

Winner: Haier for convenience; Samsung for minimalist design

3. Convertible Storage: Samsung Is More Flexible

Both refrigerators offer flexible storage, but Samsung goes further with its 5-in-1 Convertible system.

Samsung allows users to switch between different cooling configurations depending on their requirements. The freezer can be converted into refrigerator space, while certain modes can help reduce energy consumption when full storage capacity is not required.

This can be particularly useful during occasions such as festivals, family functions or periods when you need significantly more fresh-food storage than freezer space.

Haier also provides convertible storage through its Convertible Magic Zone, giving users flexibility to adapt storage according to their needs.

However, Samsung's five-mode conversion system gives it an advantage in terms of versatility.

Winner: Samsung

4. Smart Features and Wi-Fi

Both refrigerators are designed for consumers who want more than conventional cooling.

The Haier features Smart Sense AI and Wi-Fi connectivity, along with its inverter technology and convertible storage. It combines smart cooling features with practical functions such as the water dispenser.

Samsung takes its smart ecosystem further through Wi-Fi and SmartThings integration. Users can monitor the refrigerator, control certain functions, receive alerts and access connected features through the SmartThings ecosystem. Samsung also offers AI Energy Mode, which analyses usage patterns and can recommend energy-saving settings.

For households already using Samsung's connected ecosystem, this can make the refrigerator considerably more useful.

Winner: Samsung

5. Cooling Performance and Freshness

Haier uses frost-free cooling and inverter technology, making it a capable choice for everyday refrigeration. Its design also includes toughened glass shelves and dedicated storage compartments.

Samsung's Twin Cooling Plus is one of its stronger features. The system independently manages cooling conditions in the refrigerator and freezer compartments, helping maintain appropriate humidity and temperature levels. Samsung also provides Power Cool and Power Freeze functions for faster cooling and freezing.

For buyers who frequently store fresh produce and want more sophisticated cooling management, Samsung has the stronger feature set.

Winner: Samsung

6. Energy Efficiency

Both models carry a 3-star energy rating, so neither has a decisive advantage based purely on the star rating.

Both use inverter compressor technology. Haier specifies an inverter compressor, while Samsung uses a Digital Inverter Compressor.

Samsung adds another layer through its AI Energy Mode. According to Samsung, the feature can analyse usage patterns and help reduce energy consumption, with the company claiming savings of up to 10% under applicable conditions.

Actual electricity consumption will depend on usage, ambient temperature, door openings and settings, but Samsung's additional energy-management tools give it an edge.

Winner: Samsung

7. Convenience: Haier's Water Dispenser Matters

This is where Haier has a particularly practical advantage.

The built-in water dispenser makes it easier to access chilled water without opening the refrigerator door. For families that frequently drink chilled water, this is a feature that can become useful every day.

Samsung focuses more heavily on smart functionality and convertible storage rather than a built-in external water dispenser.

If convenience means quick access to drinking water, Haier deserves serious consideration.

Winner: Haier

8. Price and Value

The pricing of both models can vary considerably depending on retailer offers, discounts and availability. At the time of comparison, TheBestProducts listings showed the Haier at around ₹85,490 and the Samsung at around ₹85,990, although buyers should always check the current selling price before purchasing.

At similar promotional pricing, Samsung becomes particularly attractive because it provides more capacity and advanced 5-in-1 conversion along with SmartThings connectivity.

Haier, however, offers a strong combination of three-door organization, water dispensing, convertible storage and smart features.

Winner: Samsung for overall feature-to-capacity value

Final Verdict

Both are strong choices in the premium large-capacity refrigerator segment, but Samsung 653L wins this comparison overall.

Its extra 61 litres of capacity, 5-in-1 Convertible functionality, Twin Cooling Plus, Wi-Fi connectivity and SmartThings ecosystem make it the more feature-rich option for a modern large family.

That said, Haier 592L can be the better choice for buyers who value practical convenience over having the maximum number of smart features. The three-door design and water dispenser are genuinely useful additions, especially for families that frequently access chilled water.

In short, Samsung is the better all-rounder, while Haier is the more convenience-focused alternative. If both are available at similar prices, Samsung gets our overall recommendation. If the Haier is significantly cheaper or its water dispenser and three-door layout are priorities, it becomes an excellent value-oriented alternative.
