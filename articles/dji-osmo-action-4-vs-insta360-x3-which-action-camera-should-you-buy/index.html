DJI Osmo Action 4 vs Insta360 X3: Which Action Camera Should You Buy?

Choosing the right action camera can be surprisingly difficult. On paper, the DJI Osmo Action 4 and Insta360 X3 may appear to compete in the same category, but they are actually designed for different types of users. One focuses on conventional 4K action-camera recording, while the other takes a completely different approach with 360-degree video.

The DJI Osmo Action 4 is built for people who want sharp 4K footage, strong stabilization, excellent outdoor durability and a straightforward shooting experience. The Insta360 X3, on the other hand, is aimed at creators who want to capture everything around them and decide the framing later.

So, DJI Osmo Action 4 vs Insta360 X3 — which one is better? The answer depends on what you plan to record.

Design and Build Quality

The DJI Osmo Action 4 follows the traditional action-camera design. It has a compact rectangular body with front and rear colour displays, making it easy to frame yourself while recording. At 145g, it is also relatively lightweight for helmet, handlebar and chest mounting.

DJI rates the Osmo Action 4 for waterproofing up to 18 metres without a waterproof case, which gives it an advantage for swimming, water sports and other outdoor activities.

The Insta360 X3 has a completely different vertical design because it houses cameras on both sides of the body. Its dual-lens arrangement allows the camera to capture a 360-degree view.

The X3 weighs 180g and has a 2.29-inch touchscreen. Its lenses are exposed on either side, so users need to be more careful when placing the camera on rough surfaces.

For traditional action-camera handling, the DJI Osmo Action 4 is more straightforward. The Insta360 X3, however, offers a shooting capability that the DJI simply cannot match.

Video Quality: DJI Osmo Action 4 vs Insta360 X3

Video recording is where the biggest difference between these cameras becomes obvious.

The DJI Osmo Action 4 uses a 1/1.3-inch CMOS sensor and can record 4K video at up to 120fps. It also supports multiple 4K aspect ratios and slow-motion recording, making it particularly useful for action sequences, motorcycle rides and sports.

Its large sensor for an action camera also helps when shooting in less-than-ideal lighting conditions. The camera supports a wide 155-degree field of view and can record using H.264 or HEVC.

The Insta360 X3 takes a different route. Its main attraction is 5.7K 360-degree video. Instead of recording only what the camera is pointing toward, it captures the entire surrounding scene.

You can then use the Insta360 app to reframe the footage and choose the perspective you want. Insta360 describes this approach as essentially allowing you to shoot first and decide where to point later.

This is incredibly useful when recording unpredictable action.

For example, while riding a motorcycle, you don't need to constantly worry about whether the camera is pointing toward the road, yourself or another rider. The entire scene is captured and the framing can be selected during editing.

For conventional 4K video quality, DJI has the advantage. For creative flexibility, Insta360 X3 wins.

Stabilization for Biking and Adventure

Action cameras are often used in situations where there is plenty of movement, so stabilization is extremely important.

The DJI Osmo Action 4 uses RockSteady 3.0 electronic image stabilization. It also offers HorizonSteady, which can keep the horizon level even when the camera undergoes significant rotation. DJI specifically highlights its usefulness for activities such as cycling and other high-movement situations.

The Insta360 X3 uses FlowState Stabilization. Because the X3 records a 360-degree view, stabilization and reframing work together to create smooth footage while giving the creator more freedom over the final composition.

For someone who wants to mount a camera on a motorcycle and simply record the ride, the DJI Osmo Action 4 is easier.

For creators who want dramatic angles, third-person perspectives and unusual camera movements, the X3 can produce more interesting results.

Which Is Better for Travel?

Both cameras make excellent travel companions, but they serve different purposes.

The DJI Osmo Action 4 is ideal if you want to document your trip without spending too much time editing. You can record yourself walking through a location, capture landscapes, shoot activities and create conventional travel videos.

The front and rear displays are also useful for selfies and travel vlogging.

The Insta360 X3 is more interesting for creators who want to make visually distinctive travel content. Because it captures 360 degrees, you can record a location without worrying too much about precise framing.

The camera can also create the popular invisible-selfie-stick effect, allowing the footage to look as if it was recorded by a floating camera.

This makes the X3 particularly attractive for travel reels, YouTube videos and social-media content.

DJI Osmo Action 4 vs Insta360 X3 for Motorcycle Riding

For motorcycle riders, the decision becomes particularly interesting.

The DJI Osmo Action 4 is excellent for traditional riding footage. Mount it on the helmet, chest, motorcycle or handlebars and record your journey in 4K. Its strong stabilization helps deal with road vibration and movement.

The X3 offers a completely different experience.

With a 360-degree camera mounted on a motorcycle or an extended selfie stick, you can capture the rider, road and surrounding environment at the same time. The footage can later be reframed to create different perspectives.

This means one recording can potentially produce several different shots.

If your priority is simple, high-quality motorcycle footage, choose DJI.

If your priority is creative motorcycle videos and multiple viewpoints, choose Insta360 X3.

Battery Life and Outdoor Recording

Battery life is another important consideration for travel and adventure users.

DJI lists up to 160 minutes of operating time for the Osmo Action 4 under specific laboratory conditions: 1080p/24fps, RockSteady enabled, Wi-Fi disabled and the screen off. Actual battery life will vary depending on resolution, frame rate, stabilization and other settings.

The Insta360 X3 has an 1800mAh battery and is rated for approximately 81 minutes of runtime under the manufacturer's testing conditions.

For long motorcycle trips, trekking or full-day travel, carrying an additional battery or charging solution is recommended with either camera.

Editing Experience

This is one area where the DJI Osmo Action 4 is considerably easier.

The DJI records conventional action-camera footage. Once you transfer the video to your phone or computer, you can edit it using almost any mainstream video-editing software.

The Insta360 X3 requires a slightly different workflow.

Because the camera records a 360-degree image, you generally need to reframe the footage before producing the final video. Insta360's app provides tools for selecting viewpoints and creating conventional-looking videos from the 360-degree recording.

This adds another step, but it is also the reason the X3 can produce much more creative footage.

Therefore, DJI is better for quick editing, while Insta360 is better for creators who enjoy experimenting with their footage.

DJI Osmo Action 4 vs Insta360 X3: Final Verdict

The DJI Osmo Action 4 and Insta360 X3 are both capable action cameras, but they are built around very different ideas.

The DJI Osmo Action 4 is the better choice for people who want a reliable, conventional action camera. Its 1/1.3-inch sensor, 4K/120fps recording, RockSteady stabilization and 18-metre waterproof rating make it particularly appealing for biking, motorcycle rides, travel, trekking and adventure recording.

The Insta360 X3 is the more creative option. Its 5.7K 360-degree recording, dual 1/2-inch sensors and reframing capabilities allow you to capture an entire scene and decide on the final viewpoint later.

So, which one should you buy?

If you want simple, high-quality 4K action footage, choose the DJI Osmo Action 4.

If you want 360-degree recording, creative perspectives and greater freedom during editing, choose the Insta360 X3.

For motorcycle riders, vloggers and adventure enthusiasts who simply want a dependable action camera, the DJI Osmo Action 4 is the more practical all-round choice. For travel creators and social-media users looking to produce unique, immersive footage, the Insta360 X3 is the more exciting option.

Neither camera is universally better. The right choice ultimately depends on whether you want to capture the shot as you see it or capture everything and decide the shot later.
