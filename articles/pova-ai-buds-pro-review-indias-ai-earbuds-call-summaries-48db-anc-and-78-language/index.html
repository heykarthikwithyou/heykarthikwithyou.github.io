POVA AI Buds Pro Review: India’s AI Earbuds With Call Summaries, 48dB ANC and 78-Language Translation

POVA AI Buds Pro Review: India’s AI Earbuds With Call Summaries, 48dB ANC and 78-Language Translation
POVA AI Buds Pro Review

Wireless earbuds are no longer just about listening to music or answering phone calls. With artificial intelligence becoming part of everyday consumer technology, earbuds are increasingly being designed to help users communicate, record conversations, translate languages and manage information.

The POVA AI Buds Pro, launched in India under TECNO's POVA brand, takes that idea a step further. Rather than positioning itself simply as another premium pair of TWS earbuds, it focuses heavily on AI-powered productivity.

The earbuds combine AI-powered call and meeting summaries, transcription and action-item assistance with 48dB Hybrid Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), Hi-Res Audio, wireless charging and up to four hours of offline recording.

Another notable feature is its translation capability. While the broader POVA AI Buds Pro ecosystem is promoted with support for up to 78 languages, the India-focused experience places particular emphasis on real-time translation across 12 major Indian languages, making the feature more relevant to the country's multilingual audience.

The earbuds launched in India at a standard price of ₹14,999, while the effective introductory price was around ₹12,499 through platforms including Amazon India and Flipkart.

So, are the POVA AI Buds Pro worth considering over conventional premium TWS earbuds? Let's take a closer look.

POVA AI Buds Pro: Key Features

  • AI-powered call and meeting summaries
  • Transcription and structured meeting information
  • AI-generated action items
  • Up to 78-language translation ecosystem
  • Real-time translation focused on 12 major Indian languages
  • 48dB Hybrid ANC
  • Hi-Res Audio
  • Up to 4 hours of offline recording
  • Dedicated physical recording button on the charging case
  • Wireless charging support
  • Privacy-focused security features
  • Designed for calls, meetings, music and everyday communication

Design and Build: More Than Just a Music Accessory

At first glance, the POVA AI Buds Pro follows the familiar true wireless earbud format. But its design philosophy is different from conventional TWS products.

These earbuds are built around the idea that they can be used throughout the day for more than music. You can use them for calls, meetings, recordings, translation and entertainment.

One of the most interesting design decisions isn't actually on the earbuds themselves. The charging case features a dedicated physical button for recording.

This allows users to initiate recording directly from the case without having to unlock a smartphone or open a companion application first.

That small addition makes sense for a product focused on productivity. When you need to quickly capture something important, navigating through a phone app can be inconvenient. A dedicated physical control provides a much more immediate way of starting a recording.

AI Call and Meeting Summaries

The biggest reason to consider the POVA AI Buds Pro is its AI functionality.

Traditional earbuds essentially act as an audio interface. The POVA AI Buds Pro attempts to become an information-management tool as well.

Its AI features are designed to help turn conversations into more structured information, including transcripts, summaries and action items.

Imagine attending a business meeting where several topics are discussed, decisions are made and tasks are assigned. Instead of trying to remember everything or manually write down every point, the AI features can help organize the conversation into a more useful format.

This could be particularly helpful for:

  • Business meetings
  • Client calls
  • Interviews
  • Online meetings
  • Brainstorming sessions
  • Academic discussions
  • Work-related conversations

The action-item functionality is especially relevant to professionals. A meeting summary tells you what was discussed, while action items can help identify what needs to happen next.

Of course, AI-generated information should not automatically be treated as perfect. Names, figures, deadlines and important decisions should still be checked against the original conversation.

Dedicated Recording Button Makes a Difference

One of the most practical features of the POVA AI Buds Pro is the physical recording button built into the charging case.

This deserves attention because it separates the product from many conventional wireless earbuds.

Instead of needing to unlock your phone, find a recording application and start recording manually, the dedicated control is intended to provide quick access to recording.

The earbuds support up to four hours of offline recording, which makes the feature particularly interesting for meetings, interviews, classes and other situations where an internet connection may not always be available.

Offline recording can also be useful when you want to capture information first and process it later.

However, recording other people should always be handled responsibly. Users should follow applicable laws, workplace policies and consent requirements before recording conversations.

Translation: A Particularly Interesting Feature for India

Translation is another major selling point of the POVA AI Buds Pro.

The broader product positioning mentions support for up to 78 languages, but the India-specific implementation is particularly interesting because it emphasizes real-time audio translation across 12 major Indian languages.

This makes considerable sense for the Indian market.

India is one of the world's most linguistically diverse countries, and conversations frequently involve people who speak different regional languages. Real-time translation through earbuds could therefore have practical applications beyond international travel.

For example, the feature could potentially be useful when:

  • Travelling between Indian states
  • Communicating with customers
  • Meeting people who speak another regional language
  • Interacting during business travel
  • Visiting unfamiliar regions
  • Communicating across language barriers

For international travellers, the wider language ecosystem also adds another layer of usefulness.

The important point, however, is that language support and translation performance aren't necessarily the same thing. The quality of translation can vary depending on the language, pronunciation, background noise, conversation complexity and other conditions.

So, while the 78-language figure is impressive, users should look at the specific languages and translation modes they actually need.

48dB Hybrid ANC for Everyday Noise

The POVA AI Buds Pro also targets users who want premium audio features.

The earbuds offer 48dB Hybrid Active Noise Cancellation, designed to reduce unwanted environmental sounds.

ANC can be especially useful when travelling or working in noisy environments.

You could use it while:

  • Travelling by train or metro
  • Flying
  • Working from a busy café
  • Sitting in a shared office
  • Commuting
  • Studying in a noisy environment

Hybrid ANC uses microphones to detect external and internal sound and applies noise-cancelling processing to reduce unwanted noise.

The advertised 48dB figure gives the earbuds a strong specification on paper, but buyers should remember that real-world ANC performance depends on several factors, including ear-tip fit, environmental noise and the quality of the noise-cancellation processing.

In other words, the number alone doesn't tell the entire story.

Hi-Res Audio for Music and Entertainment

Despite all the attention surrounding AI, the POVA AI Buds Pro remains a pair of wireless earbuds first and foremost.

The earbuds support Hi-Res Audio, making them suitable for users who also care about music quality.

This is important because a product focused heavily on productivity could easily have sacrificed attention to audio performance. Here, TECNO has attempted to cover both sides of the equation.

For music listeners using good-quality audio sources, Hi-Res Audio can potentially provide greater detail and clarity.

Combined with ANC, it can also create a more immersive listening experience by reducing external distractions.

Casual listeners streaming music and podcasts may not notice every benefit of higher-resolution audio, but enthusiasts are more likely to appreciate the additional audio capabilities.

Wireless Charging Adds Convenience

The POVA AI Buds Pro also supports wireless charging.

It isn't necessarily the headline feature, but it adds convenience to a device that is likely to be used every day.

Users who already have a compatible wireless charging setup can simply place the case on the charging surface rather than reaching for a cable.

Small convenience features like this become more valuable when earbuds are used frequently for calls, meetings, travel and entertainment.

Privacy Matters More When Earbuds Record Conversations

The AI features of the POVA AI Buds Pro also raise an important issue: privacy.

Unlike ordinary earbuds, AI-enabled earbuds may interact with recordings, conversations, meeting information and potentially sensitive personal or professional content.

That makes the product's privacy-first security approach an important consideration.

Professionals in particular should understand how recordings are stored, processed and transferred, as well as what permissions the companion software requires.

The best way to use such features is to treat privacy as part of the product experience rather than an afterthought.

Before using recording or AI processing features, buyers should review the applicable privacy policies and permissions and follow the relevant rules surrounding the recording of other people.

POVA AI Buds Pro Price in India

The POVA AI Buds Pro was launched in India at a standard price of ₹14,999.

During the introductory period, the effective price was around ₹12,499, including launch offers available through platforms such as Amazon India and Flipkart.

This puts the earbuds firmly into the premium segment of India's TWS market.

At this price, consumers are not simply comparing sound quality and ANC. They are also evaluating whether the AI features provide enough additional value to justify the premium over conventional wireless earbuds.

That makes the POVA AI Buds Pro a particularly interesting proposition for productivity-focused users.

Who Should Buy the POVA AI Buds Pro?

1. Professionals

Professionals who regularly attend meetings and make business calls are among the users most likely to benefit from AI summaries, transcripts, action items and recording.

2. Students

Students could potentially use the recording and AI capabilities for lectures, discussions and study-related conversations, while following applicable rules regarding recording.

3. Frequent Travellers

The translation features could be useful for people who regularly travel between regions or countries and encounter different languages.

4. Remote Workers

People who spend much of their working day in online meetings may find the productivity-oriented features more useful than conventional TWS functions.

5. Multilingual Users

The India-focused translation capabilities make the earbuds particularly interesting for people who frequently communicate across regional language barriers.

6. Music Lovers

With Hi-Res Audio and 48dB Hybrid ANC, the earbuds can also serve as an everyday entertainment device rather than functioning purely as a productivity accessory.

Who Should Skip the POVA AI Buds Pro?

The product may not make as much sense for buyers who simply want affordable earbuds for music and occasional calls.

If you rarely attend meetings, don't need recording, don't use translation and aren't interested in AI productivity features, you may get better value from a conventional TWS model.

The POVA AI Buds Pro makes the most sense when you actually intend to use its additional capabilities.

POVA AI Buds Pro Review: Verdict

The POVA AI Buds Pro is an ambitious attempt to redefine what wireless earbuds can do.

Instead of competing solely on sound quality, ANC and battery life, TECNO's POVA sub-brand has built the product around AI-powered communication and productivity.

The combination of call and meeting summaries, transcription, action items, translation, offline recording and a dedicated physical recording button gives the earbuds a very different identity from conventional TWS products.

The hardware specifications are also competitive. 48dB Hybrid ANC, Hi-Res Audio and wireless charging ensure that the earbuds aren't sacrificing the traditional features buyers expect from a premium audio product.

The translation functionality is particularly interesting in India, where the emphasis on 12 major Indian languages gives the product a practical local angle, while the wider ecosystem supports up to 78 languages.

At ₹14,999, however, these are not impulse-buy earbuds. The value proposition depends largely on how much use you can get from the AI features.

For professionals, frequent travellers, students, remote workers and multilingual users, the POVA AI Buds Pro could be a compelling alternative to conventional premium TWS earbuds.

For someone who simply wants music and occasional calls, the additional AI functionality may not justify the higher price.

Final Rating: 4/5

Best for: Professionals, frequent travellers, students, remote workers and users who want AI-powered productivity features alongside premium audio.

Bottom line: The POVA AI Buds Pro is more than another pair of wireless earbuds. Its real appeal lies in bringing AI summaries, translation and recording into an everyday device you already wear. If those features fit your daily routine, it offers a genuinely different proposition in India's premium TWS market.
