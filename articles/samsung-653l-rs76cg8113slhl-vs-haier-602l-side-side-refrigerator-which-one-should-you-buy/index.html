Samsung 653L RS76CG8113SL/HL vs Haier 602L Side-by-Side Refrigerator: Which One Should You Buy?

By Karthik Kumar D K Published: Aug 16, 2026 9:07 AM IST
Samsung 653L RS76CG8113SL/HL vs Haier 602L Side-by-Side Refrigerator: Which One Should You Buy?
Samsung 653L RS76CG8113SL/HL vs Haier 602L Side-by-Side Refrigerator

A side-by-side refrigerator is more than just a kitchen appliance—it is a long-term investment that directly impacts food storage, energy consumption, and everyday convenience. With Indian households increasingly choosing premium refrigerators offering larger capacities and modern cooling technologies, Samsung and Haier have become two of the strongest competitors in this segment.

The Samsung 653L RS76CG8113SL/HL and the Haier 602L Frost Free Side-by-Side Convertible Refrigerator are designed for large families, frequent entertainers, and anyone who prefers organised storage without compromising on style. Although both refrigerators share several similarities, they differ in areas such as cooling technology, smart features, storage flexibility, and overall value.

If you're planning to purchase a premium refrigerator from Amazon India, this comparison will help you understand which model is better suited to your needs.

Design and Build Quality

Both refrigerators feature elegant side-by-side designs with premium metallic finishes that complement modern modular kitchens.

Samsung follows a minimalist design philosophy with flush doors, concealed handles, and a refined appearance that gives it a premium look. The interior lighting is bright, and the shelves are neatly arranged to maximise visibility.

Haier adopts a slightly bolder styling with wider storage sections and practical shelving. While it may not feel quite as luxurious as Samsung, the overall construction is sturdy and well-designed for everyday family use.

If aesthetics and premium finish are high on your priority list, Samsung takes a slight lead.

Storage Capacity

Storage is one of the biggest deciding factors when purchasing a side-by-side refrigerator.

Samsung offers a generous 653-litre capacity, making it suitable for families of five or more members. The additional space becomes particularly useful during festivals, parties, or bulk grocery shopping.

Haier provides 602 litres, which is still more than sufficient for most Indian households. Its shelves, large vegetable compartments, spacious door bins, and dedicated freezer sections ensure excellent organisation.

The difference of around 50 litres may not seem significant on paper, but buyers who regularly store large containers, party platters, or weekly grocery supplies will appreciate Samsung's extra room.

Cooling Performance

Cooling technology determines how evenly food remains fresh throughout the refrigerator.

Samsung uses All Around Cooling, where cold air circulates through multiple vents positioned across every shelf. This ensures consistent temperatures, helping fruits, vegetables, dairy products, and beverages stay fresh for longer.

Haier employs its Multi Air Flow Cooling System, which also distributes cool air efficiently throughout the cabinet. It performs well under normal household usage and recovers quickly after frequent door openings.

In day-to-day use, both refrigerators deliver reliable cooling performance. However, Samsung generally maintains slightly more consistent temperature distribution, particularly when the refrigerator is heavily loaded.

Compressor and Energy Efficiency

Both brands use inverter compressor technology, which adjusts cooling according to usage instead of operating continuously at maximum power.

Samsung's Digital Inverter Compressor is recognised for quiet operation, long-term durability, and reduced electricity consumption. It automatically adapts compressor speed depending on cooling demand, helping minimise energy usage while reducing wear and tear.

Haier's Twin Inverter Technology also offers efficient cooling with low noise levels and stable performance. It provides noticeable savings compared to conventional compressors and is well suited to Indian weather conditions.

From an efficiency perspective, both perform admirably, although Samsung has built a stronger reputation over the years for long-term compressor reliability.

Convertible Features

This is where Haier distinguishes itself.

The Haier 602L refrigerator includes convertible storage modes, allowing selected compartments to switch between freezer and refrigerator depending on seasonal requirements. During festivals or family gatherings, users can temporarily expand refrigerator space without needing an additional appliance.

Samsung focuses more on maintaining consistent cooling rather than providing convertible functionality.

For buyers who frequently require flexible storage arrangements, Haier offers a clear practical advantage.

Smart Features

Samsung has invested significantly in connected home technology.

The refrigerator supports SmartThings, allowing users to monitor appliance status, receive maintenance notifications, and manage selected functions through a smartphone. Integration with Samsung's smart ecosystem provides additional convenience for users already invested in connected devices.

Haier keeps things relatively simple. While it focuses on dependable performance, its smart capabilities remain limited compared to Samsung's ecosystem.

Tech-savvy buyers looking for a connected kitchen experience will likely prefer Samsung.

Noise Levels

Modern inverter compressors have made refrigerators considerably quieter than older models.

Samsung operates with impressively low noise, making it suitable for open kitchens and apartments where silent operation is appreciated.

Haier is also quiet during normal operation, although compressor cycling may occasionally be slightly more noticeable.

Neither refrigerator should cause concern in everyday residential environments.

Value for Money

Price often becomes the deciding factor.

Samsung commands a higher price because of its larger capacity, premium finish, SmartThings integration, and established brand reputation.

Haier, meanwhile, delivers many premium features—including convertible storage and inverter cooling—at a significantly more affordable price. Buyers seeking maximum features without stretching their budget will find Haier particularly attractive.

Final Verdict

Choosing between these two refrigerators ultimately depends on your priorities.

If you want a premium appliance with superior build quality, larger storage capacity, reliable cooling performance, smart connectivity, and a strong long-term reputation, the Samsung 653L RS76CG8113SL/HL is the better overall choice. It is particularly suitable for buyers who value advanced features and expect their refrigerator to serve them reliably for many years.

However, if your focus is on getting the best combination of capacity, modern cooling technology, convertible storage, and affordability, the Haier 602L Frost Free Side-by-Side Convertible Refrigerator delivers exceptional value. It offers many features typically found in higher-priced models while remaining accessible to budget-conscious families.

For most buyers seeking premium quality without compromise, Samsung edges ahead with its refined design, smart ecosystem, and consistently strong performance. On the other hand, shoppers looking to maximise features per rupee spent will likely find Haier to be the more practical purchase.

Both refrigerators are excellent options, and either will serve a modern Indian family well. Your final decision should depend on whether you prioritise premium technology and brand assurance or outstanding value with flexible storage features.
