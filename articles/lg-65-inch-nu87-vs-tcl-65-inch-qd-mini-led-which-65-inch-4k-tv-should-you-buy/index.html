LG 65-Inch NU87 vs TCL 65-Inch QD-Mini LED: Which 65-Inch 4K TV Should You Buy?

LG 65-Inch NU87 vs TCL 65-Inch QD-Mini LED: Which 65-Inch 4K TV Should You Buy?
LG 65-Inch NU87 vs TCL 65-Inch QD-Mini LED

A 65-inch 4K TV is a serious upgrade. It can completely change your living room, but it can also become an expensive mistake if you choose a television based only on brand reputation or a long list of features.

That is exactly why the LG 65-Inch NU87 AI Series 4K Smart TV and the TCL 65-Inch QD-Mini LED 4K Smart Google TV deserve a closer comparison.

Both promise a big-screen 4K experience, smart features and better picture processing. But they are not really competing in the same way.

LG is selling you a polished, intelligent everyday TV. TCL is selling you display performance.

And if you're spending your money on a 65-inch television, that difference matters.

The LG NU87 combines a 4K UHD display with LG's α7 AI processor, AI upscaling, HDR processing and webOS. The TCL QD-Mini LED takes a more hardware-heavy approach, combining quantum-dot colour with Mini LED backlighting, local dimming and, on applicable models, much higher refresh rates.

So which one deserves a place on your wall?

Let's get straight to it.

LG NU87 vs TCL QD-Mini LED: The Quick Verdict

Our pick for picture quality and gaming: TCL QD-Mini LED

Our pick for simple everyday family viewing: LG NU87

That may sound like a straightforward conclusion, but there's an important reason behind it.

The LG NU87's biggest advantage is its processing and overall user experience. LG's α7 AI processor is designed to improve different types of content, including lower-resolution material, while webOS keeps the smart-TV experience familiar and easy to navigate.

TCL, however, puts significantly more emphasis on the physical display. Mini LED backlighting gives it much finer control over brightness and dark areas, while quantum-dot technology is designed to deliver richer colour.

If you're watching a normal TV broadcast, the difference may not immediately blow you away.

Put on a high-quality HDR movie, however, and the TCL starts making a much stronger case.

LG 65-Inch NU87: The Smarter Everyday Choice?

The LG NU87 isn't trying to win the specification war.

Instead, it focuses on something that is easy to overlook when shopping for a TV: how enjoyable the television is to use every day.

The 65-inch 4K UHD panel provides plenty of screen real estate, while LG's α7 AI processor handles picture processing and upscaling. LG lists 4K Super Upscaling, Dynamic Tone Mapping, AI HDR Remastering and HDR10/HLG support among the television's features.

Why does that matter?

Because most people don't spend their evenings watching perfect native 4K footage.

You might watch a YouTube video recorded in Full HD, switch to a streaming service, watch a television channel and then finish the night with a 4K movie.

The NU87 is designed to handle that mixture without demanding much effort from the viewer.

That's where LG makes sense.

The webOS Advantage

LG's webOS is another major reason to consider the NU87.

The interface is clean and straightforward, and the TV supports features including Google Cast, Apple AirPlay, Apple Home integration and LG ThinQ.

For a family television, that matters more than it sounds.

A TV shouldn't require a tutorial every time someone wants to open Netflix.

If you want a big-screen television that is easy for everyone at home to use, the LG NU87 is a strong proposition.

But there's one area where LG's relatively conventional display hardware starts to show its limits.

TCL QD-Mini LED: Where the Picture Gets Serious

This is the reason TCL deserves your attention.

The TCL QD-Mini LED approach combines two important display technologies: quantum dots and Mini LED backlighting.

Quantum dots are primarily about colour. Mini LED is primarily about backlight control.

Together, they can produce a much more convincing HDR image than a conventional LED television.

Mini LED divides the backlight into many independently controlled areas. When a scene contains a bright object against a dark background, the television can keep the bright area illuminated while reducing the light behind darker portions of the image.

The practical result?

Better contrast. Deeper blacks. Brighter highlights. More impactful HDR.

And that's exactly the kind of improvement you can appreciate on a 65-inch screen.

TCL's QD-Mini LED models also offer high brightness and advanced local-dimming capabilities, although exact specifications vary between models. For example, TCL's C6K range uses QD-Mini LED technology with hundreds of local-dimming zones, while other models in TCL's range push brightness and gaming specifications even further.

This is why TCL is the television I'd lean toward if your priority is movie nights, HDR streaming and getting the most impressive picture possible.

Picture Quality: Don't Let “4K” Make the Decision

Here's an important point when comparing these TVs:

Both being 4K does not mean they will look the same.

4K tells you the resolution. It doesn't tell you how good the blacks are, how bright HDR highlights can become, how accurately the TV controls its backlight or how wide its colour range is.

This is where TCL's QD-Mini LED technology becomes significant.

The LG NU87 can process and upscale content intelligently, which is excellent for everyday viewing. But TCL has an advantage at the panel level.

For a dark scene in a movie, Mini LED's local dimming can help produce deeper blacks.

For HDR content, greater brightness can make highlights stand out more.

For colourful content, quantum-dot technology can produce a more vibrant image.

If picture quality is the first thing you notice when you sit down in front of a TV, TCL is the better bet.

Gaming: TCL Isn't Even Playing the Same Game

If you're buying this TV for a PS5, Xbox or gaming PC, pay attention here.

The LG NU87 supports useful gaming features including ALLM, Game Optimizer, HGiG and VRR. However, the native panel refresh rate is 60Hz.

That's fine for casual gaming.

But if gaming is one of the main reasons you're buying a 65-inch TV, 60Hz shouldn't be your target if a suitable higher-refresh-rate alternative is available at a similar price.

TCL's QD-Mini LED models are much more gaming-oriented. Depending on the model, features can include 144Hz VRR, Game Master functionality and AMD FreeSync support.

That can make fast-paced games noticeably smoother.

Racing games benefit.

Shooters benefit.

Sports games benefit.

PC gaming benefits even more when your hardware can actually drive higher frame rates.

For gamers, TCL wins this comparison comfortably.

What About Sound?

Don't let either TV's audio specifications be the deciding factor unless you don't plan to add external speakers.

The LG NU87 uses a 20W 2.0-channel speaker setup and adds AI Sound Pro and Dolby Atmos-related processing.

TCL's higher-end QD-Mini LED models can offer more substantial built-in audio hardware, depending on the model.

But here's our advice: if you're already spending money on a 65-inch television, consider adding a decent soundbar later.

A great picture paired with weak audio still feels underwhelming.

LG NU87 vs TCL QD-Mini LED: Which One Gives You More for Your Money?

This is where the comparison becomes interesting.

The LG NU87 makes sense if you're buying a television as an all-purpose household appliance.

You want it to look good.

You want the interface to be easy.

You want your family to be able to use it without constantly asking you how something works.

You want AI processing to improve ordinary content.

For that buyer, LG is a very sensible choice.

But if you're the kind of buyer who immediately notices picture quality — or you're upgrading specifically because you want a more cinematic viewing experience — TCL gives you more compelling display technology.

And that's ultimately where our money would go.

If you watch mostly regular television and streaming content and value a clean smart-TV experience, LG's AI processing and webOS make a compelling case.

But if you're spending this much on a large-screen TV because you want to experience movies, HDR content and games at their best, we'd give the TCL QD-Mini LED the edge.

The reason is simple: you can see the benefits of better display hardware.

A higher-quality backlight, stronger local dimming, quantum-dot colour and higher refresh-rate capabilities aren't just marketing terms. They directly affect what you see on screen.

LG NU87 vs TCL QD-Mini LED: FAQs

Is TCL QD-Mini LED better than LG NU87?

For picture quality, HDR and gaming, TCL QD-Mini LED is the stronger choice. Its Mini LED backlighting and quantum-dot technology give it an advantage in contrast, brightness and colour. The LG NU87 is better suited to buyers who prioritise an easy-to-use smart-TV experience and AI-powered picture processing.

Which is better for movies, LG NU87 or TCL QD-Mini LED?

TCL QD-Mini LED is the better choice for movies. Mini LED local dimming can produce deeper blacks and stronger contrast, while quantum-dot technology helps deliver vibrant colours. This becomes particularly noticeable with high-quality HDR movies.

Which TV is better for gaming?

TCL QD-Mini LED is generally the better gaming TV, particularly if you choose a model with a high-refresh-rate panel. The LG NU87 supports ALLM, Game Optimizer and VRR, but its native refresh rate is 60Hz.

Does LG NU87 have AI features?

Yes. The LG NU87 uses an α7 AI Processor and includes features such as 4K Super Upscaling, AI HDR Remastering, AI Concierge and other AI-powered functions. Some advanced voice and AI features require the separately purchased AI Magic Remote.

Is a 65-inch TV good for a living room?

Yes. A 65-inch 4K TV is a strong choice for a medium-to-large living room. The larger screen makes 4K movies, sports and gaming considerably more immersive than smaller 50- or 55-inch televisions.

Does TCL QD-Mini LED have better picture quality than regular LED TVs?

Generally, yes. Mini LED allows more precise control of the backlight, while QLED/quantum-dot technology is designed to improve colour reproduction. However, actual performance depends on the specific TCL model, panel and local-dimming implementation. TCL itself notes that features and specifications vary by model.

Which is better for everyday family use, LG or TCL?

LG NU87 is the safer choice for everyday family use. Its webOS interface, AI picture processing and straightforward controls make it particularly easy to use. TCL becomes the better option when picture performance and gaming are more important than simplicity.

Should I buy LG NU87 or TCL QD-Mini LED?

If you want better display performance, HDR and gaming, choose TCL QD-Mini LED. If you want a polished, easy-to-use 65-inch smart TV for everyday family entertainment, choose LG NU87.
