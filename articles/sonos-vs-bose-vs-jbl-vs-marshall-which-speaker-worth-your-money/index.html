Sonos vs Bose vs JBL vs Marshall: Which Speaker Is Worth Your Money?

Looking for a premium portable speaker without crossing the ₹17,000 mark? We put four popular options from Sonos, Bose, JBL and Marshall head-to-head to see which one actually deserves your money.

By Karthik Kumar D K Published: Sep 5, 2026 8:17 AM IST

Looking for a premium portable speaker without crossing the ₹17,000 mark? We put four popular options from Sonos, Bose, JBL and Marshall head-to-head to see which one actually deserves your money.

Sonos vs Bose vs JBL vs Marshall: Which Speaker Is Worth Your Money?
Premium portable speaker without crossing the ₹17,000 mark

Spending ₹12,000 to ₹17,000 on a portable speaker is not a small decision. At this price, you expect more than just loud sound. You want clear music, good bass, a battery that lasts, a speaker that can handle outdoor use and a design that feels worth the money.

This is where choosing the right speaker can get confusing. The Sonos Roam 2, Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen), JBL Flip 7 and Marshall Emberton II are all popular portable speakers, but each one has a different strength.

The JBL Flip 7 focuses on powerful sound, durability and value. Bose puts more attention on its sound quality and rugged design. Sonos packs smart features into a small body, while Marshall stands out with its classic design and impressive battery life.

So, which one should you actually buy?

We compared these four portable Bluetooth speakers to see what each one offers and, more importantly, which one makes the most sense for your money.

JBL Flip 7: Best Bluetooth Speaker for Value

If you want a portable speaker that does almost everything well without spending the full ₹17,000, the JBL Flip 7 is an easy recommendation.

At ₹12,999, it is the most affordable speaker in this comparison. But the lower price does not mean it feels like a basic speaker. JBL has packed it with features that make it suitable for both indoor listening and outdoor use.

The Flip 7 is particularly appealing if you enjoy music with plenty of energy. Its sound setup includes a woofer and tweeter, while JBL's AI Sound Boost technology is designed to deliver powerful sound while reducing distortion.

It is also built to handle the outdoors. The speaker has an IP68 rating for water and dust protection, making it a good companion for trips to the beach, pool or park.

Battery life is another strong point. JBL claims up to 14 hours of normal music playback, while its Playtime Boost feature can extend the advertised playtime to up to 16 hours.

You can also connect compatible JBL speakers using Auracast when you want to create a bigger sound setup.

The Flip 7 is not necessarily the speaker to choose if you are searching for the most refined audio experience possible. However, for everyday listening, travel and outdoor use, it gets a lot right.

Why buy it: Strong sound, excellent durability, good battery life and a reasonable price.

Best for: Buyers who want the best balance of price, performance and features.

Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen): Best for Sound Quality

Bose SoundLink Flex Portable Bluetooth Speaker 2nd Gen – Blue Dusk

₹16900.00 ₹16900.00 Amazon

If sound quality is the first thing you look for in a speaker, the Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen) deserves your attention.

Bose has focused heavily on making this compact speaker sound bigger than its size suggests. It delivers a full listening experience with clear vocals and strong bass, making it a good option for everything from casual listening to longer music sessions.

One useful feature is its ability to adjust the sound depending on the speaker's position. Whether you place it upright, lay it down or hang it somewhere, the speaker is designed to maintain a consistent listening experience.

The SoundLink Flex is also made for outdoor use. Its IP67 rating provides protection against dust and water, and the speaker is designed to float, which can be useful when using it around a swimming pool.

Battery life is rated at up to 12 hours. Bose also allows compatible speakers to be connected for Stereo or Party Mode.

The biggest issue is its price. At ₹16,900, it is the most expensive speaker in this comparison. That makes it harder to recommend to someone who simply wants a good portable speaker.

But if you care about sound quality and are comfortable paying extra for the Bose experience, the SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen) is a strong option.

Why buy it: Rich sound, rugged construction and a premium listening experience.

Best for: Music lovers who put sound quality ahead of price.

Sonos Roam 2: Best Compact Smart Speaker

Sonos Roam 2 Portable Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker – Black

Now 17% Off
₹17999.00 ₹14999.00 Amazon

The Sonos Roam 2 is the most different speaker in this comparison.

While the JBL, Bose and Marshall models are primarily designed around portable Bluetooth listening, the Sonos Roam 2 gives you both Bluetooth and Wi-Fi connectivity.

This makes it especially useful if you already have Sonos speakers at home. You can use Bluetooth while travelling and switch to Wi-Fi when you return home.

The Roam 2 also supports voice control, Apple AirPlay 2 and Sonos' Automatic Trueplay technology, which adjusts the sound according to its surroundings.

Its biggest advantage, however, may simply be its size. At around 0.43kg, it is the lightest speaker among these four. It can easily be carried in a backpack or small travel bag without becoming a burden.

The speaker also has an IP67 rating, making it resistant to dust and water and suitable for outdoor use.

Battery life is rated at up to 10 hours. That is lower than the JBL and Bose, and nowhere close to the Marshall Emberton II.

So, if you only want a straightforward Bluetooth speaker, the Sonos Roam 2 may not offer the best value. But if you want a compact speaker that also works as part of a smart home audio setup, it becomes much more interesting.

Why buy it: Very compact design, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and smart features.

Best for: Sonos users and buyers who want a portable smart speaker.

Marshall Emberton II: Best for Battery Life

There is one area where the Marshall Emberton II clearly beats the competition: battery life.

Marshall claims more than 30 hours of portable playtime, which is significantly longer than the other three speakers in this comparison.

That makes it a great choice for road trips, camping, holidays or simply for people who do not want to charge their speaker every night.

The Emberton II also has the classic Marshall design. Its distinctive appearance gives it a more premium and old-school feel compared with the more modern-looking JBL and Sonos speakers.

Marshall uses two full-range drivers and two passive radiators, along with its True Stereophonic technology, to create 360-degree sound. This means you do not have to sit directly in front of the speaker to enjoy the music.

It is also built to travel. The speaker has an IP67 rating for dust and water protection, so it can handle outdoor conditions without too much worry.

At ₹14,999, it sits comfortably between the cheaper JBL and more expensive Bose.

The Emberton II is therefore a particularly attractive option if battery life, design and portability are more important to you than having the newest features.

Why buy it: Excellent battery life, distinctive design and 360-degree sound.

Best for: Travellers, campers and anyone who hates charging their speaker frequently.

JBL vs Bose vs Sonos vs Marshall: Which Bluetooth Speaker Should You Buy?

The four speakers may sit within roughly the same price range, but they are aimed at slightly different buyers.

The JBL Flip 7 is the easiest choice for someone who wants a little bit of everything. It has a strong price, powerful sound, excellent water and dust resistance and good battery life.

The Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen) is more suitable for someone who cares about the quality of the sound itself. If you regularly sit down and listen to music rather than simply playing it in the background, Bose is worth considering.

The Sonos Roam 2 is the specialist option. Its small size and Wi-Fi connectivity make it more interesting for people who already have Sonos products or want smart features alongside Bluetooth.

The Marshall Emberton II is the choice for long listening sessions. Its 30-plus-hour battery life gives it a huge advantage for travel and outdoor use.

Which Is the Best Bluetooth Speaker Under ₹17,000?

For most buyers, the JBL Flip 7 is the best overall choice.

At ₹12,999, it leaves you with more money in your pocket while still giving you strong sound, good battery life, excellent durability and useful connectivity features.

However, there is no reason to automatically choose the cheapest option.

Choose the Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen) if sound quality is your biggest priority. Go for the Sonos Roam 2 if you want a compact speaker with smart features and Wi-Fi. Pick the Marshall Emberton II if battery life is the most important thing to you.

In other words, the best speaker is not necessarily the one with the highest price. It is the one that fits the way you actually use it.

Frequently Asked Questions About Bluetooth Speakers

Which is the best Bluetooth speaker under ₹17,000?

The JBL Flip 7 is the best overall choice for most buyers. It offers a strong combination of sound, battery life, durability and features at ₹12,999. The Bose, Sonos and Marshall options are worth considering if you have specific priorities.

Is JBL Flip 7 better than Bose SoundLink Flex?

It depends on your needs. The JBL Flip 7 offers better value and stronger water and dust protection, while the Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen) is a better choice if sound quality is your main priority.

Which Bluetooth speaker has the best battery life?

The Marshall Emberton II has the longest claimed battery life among these four speakers, with more than 30 hours of portable playtime.

Is Sonos Roam 2 worth buying?

Yes, especially if you already use Sonos products or want a compact speaker with both Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. If you only need a simple portable Bluetooth speaker, the JBL Flip 7 offers better value.

Which is the best portable speaker for outdoor use?

All four speakers are designed for portable and outdoor use. The JBL Flip 7 has an IP68 rating, while the Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen), Sonos Roam 2 and Marshall Emberton II have IP67 ratings.

Which speaker is the easiest to carry?

The Sonos Roam 2 is the lightest of these four speakers, making it a good option if portability is your main concern.

Is ₹12,000 to ₹17,000 a good budget for a Bluetooth speaker?

Yes. This budget gives you access to premium portable speakers from JBL, Bose, Sonos and Marshall. At this price, it is worth comparing sound quality, battery life, durability, size and connectivity instead of choosing purely based on price.

Final Verdict

The Sonos Roam 2, Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen), JBL Flip 7 and Marshall Emberton II all have a good reason to be on your shortlist.

The JBL Flip 7 is our pick for the best overall value. The Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen) is the better choice for sound-focused listeners. The Sonos Roam 2 makes sense for buyers who want smart features in a tiny package, while the Marshall Emberton II is the clear choice for exceptional battery life.

Before buying, check the latest selling price and available offers, as prices can change. But if you want a simple answer, the JBL Flip 7 gives you the most complete package without making you spend the full ₹17,000.
