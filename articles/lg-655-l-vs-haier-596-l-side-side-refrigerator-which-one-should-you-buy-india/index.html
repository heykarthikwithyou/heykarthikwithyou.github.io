LG 655 L vs Haier 596 L Side-by-Side Refrigerator: Which One Should You Buy in India?

LG 655 L vs Haier 596 L Side-by-Side Refrigerator: Which One Should You Buy in India?

Choosing a large side-by-side refrigerator is not just about picking the model with the biggest capacity. For a large family, the right refrigerator should offer enough storage, efficient cooling, convenient features, good energy performance and dependable long-term operation.

Two interesting options in this category are the LG 655 L GL-B257HWBY and the Haier 596 L HRS-682SWDU1. Both are 3-star, frost-free side-by-side refrigerators with inverter compressors, but they take slightly different approaches to storage and smart features.

The LG offers a larger 655-litre capacity along with Smart Inverter Compressor technology, Multi Air Flow and Express Freezing. The Haier has a 596-litre capacity and adds features such as Smart Sense AI, Magic Convertible functionality, an in-built water dispenser and smart connectivity.

So, which is the better refrigerator for your home? Let's compare the LG 655 L and Haier 596 L in detail.

LG lists 655 L total storage, including 416 L refrigerator and 239 L freezer capacity. Haier lists 596 L net capacity, with 392 L refrigerator and 204 L freezer capacity.

Capacity

If storage capacity is your first priority, the LG 655 L side-by-side refrigerator has an advantage.

It provides 655 litres of total storage, which is 59 litres more than the Haier. LG's configuration includes 416 litres for the refrigerator compartment and 239 litres for the freezer.

This extra space can be useful for large families that regularly store vegetables, fruits, dairy products, beverages, meal-prep containers and frozen food.

The Haier HRS-682SWDU1 offers 596 litres, divided into 392 litres of fresh-food storage and 204 litres of freezer space.

Winner for capacity: LG 655 L GL-B257HWBY

Cooling Performance

Both refrigerators use inverter compressor technology and frost-free cooling, making them suitable for everyday family use.

LG's GL-B257HWBY combines its Smart Inverter Compressor with Multi Air Flow and Linear Cooling technology. The manufacturer also provides an Express Freeze function for faster freezing when required.

Haier uses Expert Inverter Technology and Magic Cooling. It also includes Deo Fresh technology, which is designed to help maintain freshness inside the refrigerator.

For buyers who want straightforward, established cooling features, LG has a strong proposition. Haier, meanwhile, adds freshness and smart-management features that may appeal to technology-focused households.

Winner for cooling: LG 655 L GL-B257HWBY, by a narrow margin

Smart Features

This is where the comparison becomes particularly interesting.

LG's refrigerator comes with Smart Diagnosis, allowing users to identify certain issues more conveniently. It also has an internal LED display and Express Freeze functionality.

Haier takes a more connected approach with Smart Sense AI. The manufacturer lists Smart Connectivity and Smart Food Management, allowing users to monitor and manage stored food and connect to the refrigerator remotely.

For consumers specifically looking for a smart refrigerator with AI-enabled features, Haier has the more feature-rich package.

Winner for smart features: Haier 596 L HRS-682SWDU1

Convertible Storage

Another major difference is storage flexibility.

The Haier HRS-682SWDU1 features Magic Convertible functionality. This can be particularly useful when your family's storage requirements change. For example, you may need additional refrigeration space during festivals, family gatherings or periods when you purchase more fresh food.

LG's GL-B257HWBY focuses more on conventional refrigerator and freezer storage rather than convertible functionality.

Winner for flexibility: Haier 596 L HRS-682SWDU1

Water Dispenser

The Haier model comes with an in-built water dispenser, which adds convenience for families that frequently drink chilled water.

It can save you from repeatedly opening the refrigerator door simply to get a glass of water.

The LG GL-B257HWBY does not offer an equivalent built-in water dispenser. LG's official specifications indicate that the refrigerator does not require plumbing, but its ice-making system is manually operated.

Winner for convenience: Haier 596 L HRS-682SWDU1

Design and Space Requirements

Both refrigerators have a premium side-by-side appearance, but their dimensions are slightly different.

LG measures approximately 913 × 1790 × 735 mm, while Haier measures approximately 905 × 697 × 1775 mm.

The Haier is slightly narrower, shorter and shallower. That could make a difference if your kitchen has limited clearance.

LG's Western Black finish has a modern, premium appearance and can work particularly well in contemporary kitchens. Haier's Shiny Silver finish offers a more conventional stainless-steel-inspired look.

Winner for compact dimensions: Haier 596 L HRS-682SWDU1

Winner for appearance: Subjective

Energy Efficiency

Both models are listed with a 3-star energy rating, making them reasonably positioned for their large-capacity category.

Haier's published specifications list annual energy consumption at around 525 kWh, while LG's official page currently displays 539 kWh/year for this model.

Actual electricity consumption will depend on ambient temperature, usage patterns, temperature settings and how frequently the doors are opened.

For buyers focused heavily on running costs, it is worth checking the latest BEE label attached to the exact unit before purchase.

LG 655 L vs Haier 596 L: Final Verdict

Both refrigerators are strong options, but they are designed with slightly different priorities.

The LG 655 L GL-B257HWBY is the better choice if your priority is maximum storage, a spacious freezer, dependable cooling features and a premium overall refrigerator experience. Its additional 59 litres of capacity can be meaningful for large Indian families, especially those who buy groceries in bulk.

The Haier 596 L HRS-682SWDU1, on the other hand, is more attractive for buyers who want smart features, convertible storage and an in-built water dispenser. Its Smart Sense AI and connectivity features give it an edge for consumers who want their refrigerator to offer more than conventional cooling.

Our Pick

Best overall for storage and family use: LG 655 L GL-B257HWBY

Best for smart features and flexibility: Haier 596 L HRS-682SWDU1

If you have a large family and frequently need maximum refrigerator and freezer space, LG is the safer overall pick. If convenience, smart functionality and convertible storage are more important than maximum capacity, Haier deserves serious consideration.

Before buying, compare the current selling price, warranty, installation terms and after-sales service available in your location, as online refrigerator prices and offers can change frequently.
