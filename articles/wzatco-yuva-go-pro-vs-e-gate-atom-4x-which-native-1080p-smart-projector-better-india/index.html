WZATCO Yuva Go Pro vs E GATE Atom 4X+: Which Native 1080p Smart Projector Is Better

Choosing a budget smart projector can be surprisingly difficult. On paper, many projectors promise 1080p resolution, 4K support, Android, automatic focus, large-screen projection and wireless connectivity. But the actual experience depends on how these features come together in everyday use.

Two interesting options in this segment are the WZATCO Yuva Go Pro Smart Projector and the E GATE Atom 4X+ Smart Projector. Both are native 1080p projectors with automatic setup, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth and smart features, making them suitable for home entertainment, movies, sports and casual gaming.

The WZATCO Yuva Go Pro stands out with its rotatable design, automatic focus and 4D keystone correction, while the E GATE Atom 4X+ focuses on a compact design, a more clearly defined 400 ISO brightness rating and integrated smart-TV features.

So, which one should you buy? Let's compare the two projectors in detail.

The two projectors are remarkably close in their core specifications. Both deliver native Full HD resolution, so buyers are not choosing between 720p and 1080p. The bigger differences are brightness claims, software experience, physical design and connectivity.

Picture Quality: A Close Contest

The biggest advantage of both projectors is their native 1080p resolution. This is important because native resolution determines the actual resolution produced by the projector rather than simply the maximum resolution of content it can accept.

The WZATCO Yuva Go Pro delivers native 1920 × 1080p resolution and supports 4K content. Its manufacturer also highlights a 6000:1 contrast ratio and enhanced brightness.

The E GATE Atom 4X+ also offers native 1080p resolution and 4K HDR support. Its 400 ISO-lumen rating is a more standardized way of describing projector brightness than the extremely large lumen numbers commonly advertised by budget projector manufacturers.

For movies and streaming at night, both can provide a satisfying big-screen experience. However, neither should be treated as a replacement for a bright television in a sunlit room. Projectors generally perform best when ambient light is controlled.

Winner: Tie for resolution, with E GATE having an advantage in clarity around its brightness specification.

Automatic Setup and Ease of Use

This is where both projectors become particularly appealing.

The WZATCO Yuva Go Pro comes with automatic focus and automatic 4D keystone correction. That means the projector can automatically adjust focus and correct the image when the projector is not perfectly aligned with the screen.

The E GATE Atom 4X+ also provides automatic focus and keystone correction. This makes it easier to move the projector around the room without manually adjusting the image every time.

For someone who frequently changes the projector's position, these automatic features are much more useful than they might initially appear.

Winner: Tie.

Design and Flexibility

The WZATCO Yuva Go Pro has one particularly useful feature: its rotatable design. The projector can be repositioned for different viewing angles, making it useful when you want to project onto a wall, screen or even a ceiling.

This flexibility makes the Yuva Go Pro interesting for bedrooms, living rooms and temporary setups where a conventional fixed projector position isn't practical.

The E GATE Atom 4X+ takes a compact and portable approach. Its design is intended to make the projector easier to position and carry around, while its automatic correction features reduce the effort required to get a usable image.

Winner: WZATCO Yuva Go Pro, especially if flexible projection angles are important.

Smart Features and Connectivity

Both projectors are designed to work as standalone entertainment devices rather than requiring a separate streaming device for basic use.

The WZATCO Yuva Go Pro runs Android 13 and includes Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth, screen mirroring and HDMI ARC connectivity. The manufacturer's specifications also mention support for 4K HDR content and built-in smart functionality.

The E GATE Atom 4X+ offers Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth and HDMI ARC/eARC/CEC support. It also integrates Android-based smart features and Whale TV+ Live TV, giving it an additional focus on television-style entertainment.

For users who plan to connect a soundbar or external audio system, the E GATE's HDMI eARC/CEC support is particularly attractive.

Winner: E GATE Atom 4X+, particularly for users who want more advanced HDMI audio connectivity.

Brightness: Look Beyond the Numbers

Brightness is probably the area where buyers need to be most careful.

The WZATCO Yuva Go Pro is marketed with very high lumen figures, but TheBestProducts' existing review appropriately notes that actual brightness can vary from manufacturer claims and that the projector is best suited to dim lighting conditions.

E GATE specifies the Atom 4X+ at 400 ISO lumens. This doesn't necessarily mean it will look dramatically brighter or darker than a projector advertising thousands of "lumens," because projector brightness measurements are not always directly comparable across different measurement systems.

For practical use, both projectors are better suited to evenings, dark bedrooms and rooms where curtains can reduce ambient light.

Winner: E GATE Atom 4X+ for specification transparency.

Audio Performance

Neither projector should be purchased primarily for its built-in speakers.

The WZATCO Yuva Go Pro includes built-in audio, but TheBestProducts notes that its audio performance is average.

The E GATE Atom 4X+ also provides built-in speakers, but its stronger connectivity options make it easier to connect external audio equipment.

For casual YouTube viewing or regular television content, built-in speakers can be sufficient. For movies, however, adding a soundbar or Bluetooth speaker can significantly improve the experience.

Winner: E GATE Atom 4X+.

Which Projector Offers Better Value?

At the prices shown on TheBestProducts when these reviews were published, the WZATCO Yuva Go Pro was listed at ₹12,990, while the E GATE Atom 4X+ was listed at ₹10,990. Prices can change depending on offers and sellers.

That makes the E GATE particularly attractive for buyers working with a tighter budget.

The WZATCO justifies its higher price with its rotating design, automatic setup, native 1080p display, Android 13 and broad connectivity. The E GATE, meanwhile, offers a strong combination of native Full HD resolution, automatic adjustment, 400 ISO brightness, smart features and compact portability.
