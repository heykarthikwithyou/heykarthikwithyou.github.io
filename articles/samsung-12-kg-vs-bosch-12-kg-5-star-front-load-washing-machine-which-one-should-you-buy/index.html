Samsung 12 kg vs Bosch 12 kg 5 Star Front Load Washing Machine

Buying a 12 kg front load washing machine makes sense if you have a large family, wash bulky clothes regularly, or simply want to reduce the number of laundry cycles every week. Two interesting options in this category are the Samsung WW12DG6B24ASTL and Bosch WGA27400IN.

Both washing machines offer a 12 kg capacity, 5-star energy rating, 1400 RPM spin speed and an in-built heater. However, they take different approaches to washing. Samsung focuses heavily on AI-based controls, connected features, AI Ecobubble technology and faster washing, while Bosch puts more emphasis on water management, fabric care, washing programs and its EcoSilence Drive motor.

So, which one makes more sense for your home? Let's compare the Samsung 12 kg and Bosch 12 kg front load washing machines in detail.

Capacity and Overall Performance

There is no difference in rated capacity here. Both the Samsung WW12DG6B24ASTL and Bosch WGA27400IN come with a 12 kg washing capacity, making them suitable for large households and heavier laundry loads.

A 12 kg machine can also be useful when washing bedsheets, towels, blankets and other bulky items. Samsung uses its SpaceMax design to provide a large internal washing capacity while retaining a standard 60 cm wide cabinet. Bosch, meanwhile, uses a listed 66-litre drum on the WGA27400IN.

Both machines also offer a maximum spin speed of 1400 RPM, which helps remove more water from clothes during the spin cycle and can reduce drying time.

Samsung's AI Ecobubble Technology

Samsung's biggest advantage is the number of smart washing features built into the machine.

The WW12DG6B24ASTL comes with AI Ecobubble, which mixes detergent with water and air to create bubbles before and during washing. Samsung says the technology can improve cleaning in cooler water while helping with fabric care and energy consumption under specified test conditions.

The machine also has AI Control, which can learn usage patterns and provide cycle suggestions. Through Samsung SmartThings, users can connect the washing machine to a smartphone and access features such as cycle recommendations, laundry planning and troubleshooting.

For people who enjoy smart home appliances and want more control through their phone, Samsung's connected features are a significant attraction.

Bosch AI ActiveWater Plus and Fabric Care

Bosch takes a different route with the WGA27400IN.

Its AI ActiveWater Plus technology is designed to adjust water usage according to the load. This is particularly useful because washing machines do not always need the same amount of water for every load.

Bosch also includes its SoftCare Drum and paddles, which are designed to provide gentler treatment of fabrics. Anti-tangle, anti-vibration, foam control and balance control features are also listed for this model.

The Bosch machine comes with 14 wash programs, including options such as Steam Anti Bacteria, Quick Wash, Quilt/Blanket, Shirts, Jeans/Dark Wash, Cottons, Synthetics, Daily Mix, Wool, Delicates/Silk and Drum Descale.

This makes the Bosch a more straightforward choice for buyers who prefer having dedicated programs for different types of clothing rather than relying heavily on app-based features.

Washing Speed: Samsung Has an Interesting Advantage

If laundry speed is important, Samsung has a feature that stands out.

The WW12DG6B24ASTL includes Super Speed, which Samsung specifies can complete a wash in around 39 minutes with a 5 kg load under the default Super Speed settings.

This doesn't mean a full 12 kg load will always finish in 39 minutes. The actual washing time depends on the selected program, load, fabric type and settings. Still, for everyday smaller loads, Super Speed can be useful when you need clothes washed quickly.

Bosch also provides Quick Wash options, including Quick 15/30-minute programs according to the listed program information, but its overall feature approach is more focused on having different dedicated cycles.

Hygiene and Steam Washing

Both models include steam-related hygiene features, although the branding is different.

Samsung offers Hygiene Steam, which uses steam from the bottom of the drum. Samsung states that its Hygiene Steam cycle can remove up to 99.9% of certain bacteria under specified test conditions.

Bosch uses Steam Anti Bacteria, giving users a dedicated program aimed at hygienic washing.

For households that frequently wash children's clothes, towels, bedding or garments where hygiene is a priority, having a dedicated steam-based option is useful.

Motor and Long-Term Use

Samsung uses its Digital Inverter Technology, while Bosch uses an EcoSilence Drive brushless motor.

Samsung says its Digital Inverter motor is designed for efficient and quiet operation and lists a 10-year parts warranty on the inverter motor on its product information.

Bosch's EcoSilence Drive is also designed as an energy-efficient brushless motor, and the WGA27400IN is listed with a 12-year motor warranty alongside a 2-year product warranty in current product listings.

Warranty terms can change depending on the market, offer and purchase channel, so buyers should verify the applicable warranty at the time of purchase.

Smart Features: Samsung Takes a Different Approach

This is one of the clearest differences between the two machines.

Samsung includes Wi-Fi connectivity and SmartThings integration. Users can connect the washing machine to the app and access smart features from their phone.

Bosch WGA27400IN does not list Wi-Fi or app control among its smart features.

That doesn't make Bosch less capable as a washing machine. It simply means Samsung is aimed more towards buyers who appreciate connected appliances, while Bosch keeps the experience more traditional and focused on the washing machine itself.

Design and Dimensions

Samsung's WW12DG6B24ASTL comes in a Navy finish and measures approximately 60 × 85 × 60 cm. Its AI Control panel gives it a modern appearance.

Bosch WGA27400IN has a White finish and measures approximately 598 × 848 × 590 mm. Its slightly shallower listed depth may be worth considering if available installation space is limited.

Before purchasing either model, measure the complete installation area, including space required for hoses, ventilation and opening the door.

Final Verdict

The Samsung WW12DG6B24ASTL and Bosch WGA27400IN are both capable 12 kg 5-star front load washing machines, but their feature sets are quite different.

Samsung stands out with its AI Ecobubble technology, Wi-Fi connectivity, SmartThings support, AI Control, Super Speed and Hygiene Steam. It is an appealing option for buyers who want a modern, connected washing machine with plenty of smart features.

Bosch, on the other hand, offers AI ActiveWater Plus, a 66-litre drum, SoftCare Drum and paddles, 14 wash programs, Steam Anti Bacteria and an EcoSilence Drive motor. It is particularly interesting for buyers who want a large-capacity machine with a broad range of conventional washing programs and fabric-care features.

For a large Indian household, both models provide the key fundamentals: 12 kg capacity, 5-star energy rating, 1400 RPM spin speed and an in-built heater. The deciding factor is therefore less about capacity and more about the type of washing experience you prefer—Samsung's connected AI ecosystem or Bosch's program-focused and fabric-care approach.
