F Gear Hunter 75L vs Wildcraft 45L Rucksack: Which One Should You Buy?

F Gear Hunter 75L vs Wildcraft 45L Rucksack: Which One Should You Buy?
F Gear Hunter 75L vs Wildcraft 45L Rucksack

Choosing a good backpack is important when you are going on a trip, trekking in the hills, or travelling for a few days. A backpack should have enough space for your things, be comfortable to carry, and be strong enough to handle outdoor use.

Two backpacks that are worth comparing are the F Gear Hunter 75L Rucksack and the Wildcraft 45L Rucksack. Both are made for travel and trekking, but they are quite different in size.

The F Gear Hunter has a large 75-litre capacity, while the Wildcraft has a 45-litre capacity. This means the F Gear Hunter can carry much more, while the Wildcraft is smaller and easier to carry.

So, which one is better? Let's compare the two backpacks in simple terms.

F Gear Hunter 75L Rucksack

The F Gear Hunter 75L Rucksack is made for people who need a lot of space. With 75 litres of storage, you can carry clothes, shoes, food, trekking items and other things needed for a long trip.

This large size can be very useful if you are going camping or trekking for several days. You do not have to worry too much about running out of space.

One useful feature is the separate shoe compartment. You can keep your shoes away from your clothes and other items. This is a small feature, but it can be very helpful when travelling.

The backpack also comes with a rain cover. If you are travelling during the rainy season, this can help protect your belongings from getting wet.

Another useful feature is the laptop space. The F Gear Hunter can hold a laptop of up to 17 inches, according to the product information. This makes it useful for people who work while travelling.

The backpack also has padded shoulder straps, a waist belt and a chest strap. These features help spread the weight across your body.

The main thing to remember is that a 75L backpack can become heavy when you fill it completely. It is a big backpack, so it may not be the best choice for someone who likes to travel with only a few things.

Wildcraft 45L Rucksack

The Wildcraft 45L Rucksack is smaller than the F Gear Hunter, but that is also one of its biggest advantages.

With 45 litres of space, it is suitable for short trips, weekend travel, hiking and smaller trekking trips. If you do not need to carry a lot of equipment, a 45L backpack can be easier to manage.

The Wildcraft backpack has padded shoulder straps and a padded back panel. It also has a chest strap and waist belt. These features can make the backpack more comfortable when you are walking for a long time.

There are also side pockets where you can keep a water bottle or other small items. The external pocket can be used for things that you want to reach quickly.

The backpack is made from water-resistant nylon. However, unlike the F Gear Hunter, it does not come with a rain cover. This is something to keep in mind if you often travel in places where it rains.

The Wildcraft 45L is a good choice for people who want a simple backpack that is not too large.

Which Backpack Has More Space?

There is a clear winner here.

The F Gear Hunter 75L has 30 litres more storage than the Wildcraft 45L. That is a big difference.

If you are going on a long trip and need to carry many clothes, shoes, food and other items, the F Gear Hunter will give you much more room.

The Wildcraft 45L is better when you want to pack less. It can be enough for a weekend trip or a short trekking adventure.

So, if storage is your main concern, F Gear Hunter 75L is the better choice.

Which Backpack Is Easier to Carry?

This is where the Wildcraft 45L has an advantage.

A smaller backpack is usually easier to handle, especially when you are walking through busy places or going on a short trek.

The Wildcraft has padded straps, a padded back and a waist belt to help make carrying more comfortable.

The F Gear Hunter also has padded straps, a waist belt and a chest strap. These are useful because the backpack can carry a much larger load.

However, if you fill a 75L backpack completely, it can become quite heavy. For this reason, the Wildcraft may be the better option for people who prefer to travel light.

Which One Is Better for Trekking?

The answer depends on the type of trekking you do.

For long trekking trips, the F Gear Hunter 75L is the better option. Its large storage space allows you to carry more clothes, food and trekking equipment.

It also has a rain cover, which is useful when you are outdoors.

For short treks, the Wildcraft 45L can be a better choice. You do not need a very large backpack for a one-day or weekend trip.

A smaller backpack can also encourage you to carry only the things you really need.

Which One Is Better for Travel?

For long travel, the F Gear Hunter has an advantage because of its large storage space. The separate shoe compartment and rain cover are also useful when travelling.

If you are going on a short holiday, the Wildcraft 45L may be enough. Its smaller size makes it easier to carry and store.

Think about how long your trips normally are before choosing a backpack.

If you often travel for several days, go for the F Gear Hunter.

If most of your trips are short, the Wildcraft may be all you need.

F Gear Hunter 75L or Wildcraft 45L: Which One Should You Buy?

The right backpack depends on your needs.

If you need a large trekking backpack for long trips, the F Gear Hunter 75L is the better choice. It gives you plenty of space and has useful features such as a shoe compartment and rain cover.

If you want a lightweight backpack for short trips, hiking or weekend travel, the Wildcraft 45L is a better fit. It is smaller, easier to manage and still gives you enough room for the basic things you need.

In simple words, choose the F Gear Hunter 75L if you need more space, and choose the Wildcraft 45L if you want a smaller and easier-to-carry backpack.

Neither backpack is perfect for everyone. The best one is the backpack that matches the kind of trips you usually take.
