Livpure Fenix 90 cm vs INALSA Zylo 60 cm: Power, Features and Value Compared

Compare suction power, size, auto-clean, controls, warranty and features to find the right kitchen chimney for your home.

By Karthik Kumar D K Published: Aug 27, 2026 9:03 AM IST

Compare suction power, size, auto-clean, controls, warranty and features to find the right kitchen chimney for your home.

Livpure Fenix 90 cm vs INALSA Zylo 60 cm: Power, Features and Value Compared
Livpure Fenix 90 cm vs INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC 60 cm

Choosing a kitchen chimney is not just about looking at the suction capacity or picking the model with the longest warranty. The right chimney needs to match the size of your cooktop, the amount of cooking you normally do, the type of food you prepare and, of course, the space available in your kitchen.

Two models that are worth comparing in this segment are the Livpure Fenix 90 cm and the INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC. Both are filterless chimneys with auto-clean functionality and modern controls, but they are designed for somewhat different requirements.

The Livpure Fenix is a larger 90 cm T-shaped chimney with a higher 1400 m³/hr suction capacity. It also brings gesture control, motion sensing, thermal auto-clean and dual LED lighting. The INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC is a more compact 60 cm curved-glass chimney with 1250 m³/hr suction, touch and motion controls, thermal auto-clean and three speed settings.

So, which one should you buy? Let's compare them in detail.

The specifications show that both chimneys cover the important features expected in a modern Indian kitchen, but their biggest difference is size. The Livpure is 90 cm wide, while the INALSA is 60 cm. That difference matters more than it may initially appear.

Design and Size

The first thing you need to consider is the width of your cooking platform.

The Livpure Fenix is a 90 cm T-shaped chimney, making it better suited to wider cooktops and larger kitchen setups. Its dimensions are listed as 48D x 90W x 55.5H cm, and the black finish gives it a fairly contemporary appearance.

The INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC, on the other hand, is a 60 cm curved-glass chimney designed for 2-4 burner wall-mounted cooktops. INALSA specifically lists it for kitchens of around 175 sq ft and medium to heavy frying or grilling.

This makes the choice fairly straightforward.

If you have a 60 cm, two- or three-burner cooktop and limited wall space, the INALSA is the more natural fit. If you have a wider four-burner cooktop or want the chimney to cover a larger cooking area, the 90 cm Livpure Fenix has the advantage.

Winner for larger kitchens: Livpure Fenix 90 cm

Winner for compact kitchens: INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC

Suction Capacity

This is another area where the Livpure Fenix has a clear advantage on paper.

The Fenix offers 1400 m³/hr maximum suction, compared with 1250 m³/hr on the INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC. Livpure positions the Fenix for strong removal of smoke, odours and cooking fumes, while INALSA specifies its Zylo 60BKMAC for medium and heavy frying/grilling.

The difference is not enormous, but it can become useful if you regularly cook foods that generate a lot of smoke and oil vapour.

Indian cooking can involve deep frying, tadka, grilling, roasting and high-heat cooking, all of which can put more demand on a chimney. In such situations, the Fenix's combination of a larger 90 cm body and 1400 m³/hr suction makes it the more powerful option.

However, don't assume that higher suction automatically means a better chimney for every kitchen. A 60 cm cooktop in a compact kitchen doesn't necessarily need a 90 cm chimney.

Winner: Livpure Fenix 90 cm

Filterless Technology

Both models use filterless technology, which is a major convenience for homeowners who don't want to regularly remove and scrub traditional filters.

With a conventional filter chimney, accumulated grease can gradually affect airflow and the filter itself needs regular cleaning. Filterless models are designed to reduce this maintenance burden.

The Livpure Fenix combines its filterless design with a thermal auto-clean system and an oil collector. Livpure says the thermal auto-clean function helps with maintenance and durability.

The INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC also uses filterless technology and thermal auto-clean. INALSA describes its system as helping keep oil from remaining inside the cooker hood.

For everyday users, both are considerably more convenient than having to deal with removable grease filters.

Winner: Tie

Auto-Clean Performance

Auto-clean is becoming one of the most useful features in modern kitchen chimneys, particularly for households that cook frequently.

The Livpure Fenix uses thermal auto-clean technology. The heating mechanism helps deal with accumulated oil and residue inside the chimney, reducing the amount of manual cleaning required.

The INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC also comes with thermal auto-clean, along with a delay power-off function designed to help clear remaining smoke and odour after cooking.

This is an area where neither product has a major disadvantage. Both offer the basic convenience that most buyers are looking for in a modern filterless chimney.

Winner: Tie

Controls

The control system is another area where both models move away from traditional push-button operation.

The Livpure Fenix features touch and gesture/motion control. This can be particularly useful when your hands are wet, oily or occupied while cooking. Instead of touching the chimney, you can use a hand gesture to operate it. Livpure specifically highlights gesture control as one of the Fenix's features.

The INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC also offers touch control and motion sensing, giving it a similarly modern approach to everyday operation.

For this reason, there isn't a dramatic difference between the two.

Winner: Tie

Noise Levels

Noise is something buyers often overlook until they actually start using a chimney.

INALSA officially lists the maximum noise level of the Zylo 60BKMAC at 65 dB.

Livpure promotes the Fenix as a low-noise chimney, but the manufacturer's current product page does not provide the same clearly stated maximum dB figure in the specifications we found.

Therefore, it would be unfair to declare one definitively quieter based purely on the available specifications.

If a precise published noise figure is important to you, INALSA has the advantage in terms of specification transparency.

Winner: INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC, for clearly stated noise specifications

Warranty

Warranty is particularly important for a kitchen chimney because the motor is one of its most important components.

The Livpure Fenix listing supplied for this comparison carries a 10-year motor warranty and 2-year comprehensive warranty.

The INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC officially lists a 7-year warranty on the motor and 2 years on the product.

That gives Livpure an advantage on motor coverage.

A three-year difference may not matter to everyone, but for someone planning to keep the chimney for many years, longer motor coverage can provide additional peace of mind.

Winner: Livpure Fenix 90 cm

Which Chimney Is Better for Indian Cooking?

This is where the decision becomes more practical.

If your kitchen sees a lot of frying, tadka, grilling and high-heat cooking, suction power and coverage become important. The Livpure Fenix's 1400 m³/hr capacity and wider 90 cm design make it particularly attractive for larger cooking areas.

The INALSA Zylo isn't underpowered, though. Its 1250 m³/hr suction is substantial for a 60 cm chimney, and INALSA itself positions the model for medium and heavy frying/grilling.

For a typical 60 cm cooktop, therefore, you don't necessarily need to spend more simply because another model has a higher suction rating.

For heavy cooking and larger cooktops: Livpure Fenix 90 cm

For everyday cooking on a 60 cm cooktop: INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC

Which One Should You Buy?

There isn't one universal winner here because these two chimneys target slightly different requirements.

Choose the Livpure Fenix 90 cm if you have a large cooktop, cook frequently, regularly prepare fried or high-smoke foods, or simply want more coverage and higher suction capacity. Its 1400 m³/hr suction, 90 cm width, filterless design, thermal auto-clean and long motor warranty make it the more feature-rich choice for a larger kitchen.

Choose the INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC if you have a 60 cm cooktop and want a compact chimney with strong suction, auto-clean, touch control and motion sensing. Its 1250 m³/hr suction is still more than adequate for many home kitchens, and the 7-year motor plus 2-year product warranty adds confidence.

Our Verdict

Overall Winner: Livpure Fenix 90 cm

The Livpure Fenix wins this comparison because it offers the stronger combination of higher suction, wider coverage, modern controls, filterless technology, thermal auto-clean and longer motor warranty. It is the better pick if performance and coverage are your priorities.

However, the INALSA Zylo 60BKMAC is arguably the smarter choice for a compact kitchen. If your cooktop is 60 cm and you don't need the extra coverage offered by a 90 cm chimney, the Zylo gives you most of the important modern features without requiring a larger installation space.
