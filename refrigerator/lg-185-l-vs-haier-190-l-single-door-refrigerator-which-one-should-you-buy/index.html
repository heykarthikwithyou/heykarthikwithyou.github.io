LG 185 L vs Haier 190 L: What Is the Difference?

The LG 185 L 4 and Haier 190 L 3 are aimed at buyers who want a compact single-door refrigerator without moving to a larger double-door model. Both use direct-cool refrigeration and sit in a similar capacity category, making them suitable for small families, couples, bachelors or households that do not need a large freezer section.

The difference is in the approach.

LG focuses more on energy efficiency with a 4 Star rating and adds a Smart Inverter compressor. The GLD1945ZOSE also comes with a base stand with drawer and has a distinctive Scarlet Euphoria finish.

Haier, on the other hand, offers slightly more storage at 190 litres and puts more emphasis on everyday convenience with fast ice making, a large vegetable box and an easy-clean back. It is also a newer 2026 model.

So, while these two refrigerators look similar on paper, they target slightly different priorities.

1. Capacity: Haier Has Slightly More Space

Capacity is one of the first things to consider when buying a single-door refrigerator.

The Haier HED-203TDSA-N offers 190 litres, while the LG GLD1945ZOSE has 185 litres. The five-litre difference is not huge, but Haier does provide slightly more overall storage.

For a couple or small family, both capacities should be practical for everyday groceries, vegetables, dairy products, beverages and frozen items. The difference becomes more noticeable only if you regularly store larger quantities of food.

The Haier also specifically lists a large vegetable box, which can make the available storage more useful for households that buy fresh vegetables regularly.

Capacity advantage: Haier

2. Energy Efficiency: LG Takes the Lead

This is one of the biggest differences between the two refrigerators.

The LG GLD1945ZOSE carries a 4 Star energy rating, while the Haier HED-203TDSA-N has a 3 Star rating.

For an appliance that remains switched on throughout the day, energy efficiency matters. A higher BEE star rating generally indicates better energy efficiency under the applicable rating conditions.

LG also uses a Smart Inverter compressor, which is designed around efficient and stable refrigerator operation.

That makes the LG particularly interesting for buyers who are more concerned about long-term electricity consumption and want an energy-efficient single-door refrigerator.

The actual electricity bill will still depend on usage, ambient temperature, door-opening frequency and other factors, but the 4 Star rating gives LG an advantage on the specification sheet.

Energy efficiency advantage: LG

3. Cooling and Ice Making: Haier Focuses on Speed

Both refrigerators use direct-cool technology, so buyers should not expect the automatic defrosting convenience found in frost-free double-door refrigerators.

Where Haier stands out is its fast ice-making feature.

For Indian households that frequently need ice for drinks or everyday use, faster ice production can be a useful convenience. It is a relatively simple feature, but one that can make a difference in daily usage.

LG's Smart Inverter compressor, meanwhile, is more focused on efficient compressor operation and maintaining cooling performance.

Therefore, the choice depends on what matters more: LG's emphasis on inverter-based efficiency or Haier's emphasis on faster ice making.

Cooling convenience advantage: Haier

4. Storage and Vegetable Compartment

Storage organisation becomes important in a compact refrigerator because every litre counts.

Haier specifically highlights a large vegetable box in the HED-203TDSA-N. This should appeal to households that regularly store vegetables, fruits and other fresh produce.

The LG 185 L 4 GLD1945ZOSE also provides practical storage in a compact single-door format, while its base stand with drawer adds an additional storage area outside the main refrigerator compartment.

The base stand is particularly interesting because it can provide a convenient place to keep items that do not necessarily need refrigeration.

If your priority is vegetable storage inside the refrigerator, Haier's large vegetable box is a useful advantage. If you prefer additional lower storage through a stand-and-drawer setup, LG's configuration may be more appealing.

Storage advantage: Depends on your usage

5. Everyday Convenience: Haier Has Some Useful Extras

Haier's HED-203TDSA-N includes two practical features that stand out for everyday use: fast ice making and an easy-clean back.

The easy-clean back is particularly useful because the rear section of a refrigerator can collect dust over time. A design that makes cleaning easier can reduce maintenance effort.

The large vegetable box also adds to the everyday practicality.

LG counters with its Smart Inverter compressor and base stand with drawer. These features are less about adding individual conveniences and more about making the refrigerator efficient and practical for regular household use.

For buyers who want several small convenience features, Haier has a compelling specification list.

Convenience advantage: Haier

6. Design and Appearance

The two models also take different approaches to appearance.

LG offers the GLD1945ZOSE in Scarlet Euphoria, giving it a more distinctive look than the typical silver or grey refrigerator finish. It can work well for buyers who want their appliance to add some colour to the kitchen.

Haier's Dazzle Steel finish takes a more conventional modern-appliance approach. It is likely to blend more easily with different kitchen interiors.

Neither approach is objectively better. The choice comes down to personal preference.

If you like a more expressive finish, LG's Scarlet Euphoria stands out. If you prefer a traditional metallic appearance, Haier's Dazzle Steel may fit your kitchen more naturally.

7. LG vs Haier: Which One Makes More Sense for Small Families?

For a small family, both refrigerators provide a sensible amount of storage without taking up the space associated with larger double-door models.

The LG's biggest attraction is its 4 Star rating and Smart Inverter compressor. This makes it particularly relevant for buyers who intend to use the refrigerator for several years and want to pay attention to energy efficiency.

Haier provides five litres more capacity and adds features such as fast ice making, a large vegetable box and an easy-clean back.

This means the Haier is more focused on practical everyday features, while LG puts greater emphasis on energy efficiency.

LG 185 L vs Haier 190 L: Which Refrigerator Should You Buy?

The answer depends largely on what you expect from your refrigerator.

Choose the LG 185 L if:

  • You want a 4 Star refrigerator.
  • Energy efficiency is a priority.
  • You prefer an inverter compressor.
  • You like the Scarlet Euphoria finish.
  • You want a base stand with drawer.
  • You are comfortable with slightly less capacity in exchange for the higher energy rating.

Choose the Haier 190 L if:

  • You want slightly more storage capacity.
  • Fast ice making is important to you.
  • You prefer a large vegetable box.
  • You want an easy-clean back design.
  • You prefer the Dazzle Steel finish.
  • You want a newer 2026 model.

Final Verdict

The LG 185 L and Haier 190 L are both practical choices for buyers looking for a compact direct-cool single-door refrigerator, but their strengths are different.

The LG 185 L 4 GLD1945ZOSE stands out for its 4 Star energy rating, Smart Inverter compressor and base stand with drawer. It makes more sense for buyers who place energy efficiency high on their list and want a stylish refrigerator with a distinctive finish.

The Haier 190 L 3 HED-203TDSA-N offers slightly more capacity, fast ice making, a large vegetable box and an easy-clean back. These features make it a practical option for households looking for everyday convenience and a little extra storage.

For energy-conscious buyers, the LG's 4 Star rating is the key attraction. For buyers who value storage and convenience features, the Haier has plenty to offer.

Ultimately, neither refrigerator is a one-size-fits-all choice. The better option depends on whether your priority is energy efficiency with LG or additional capacity and convenience with Haier.
