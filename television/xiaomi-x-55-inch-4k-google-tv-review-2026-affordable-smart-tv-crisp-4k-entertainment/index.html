Xiaomi X 55-Inch 4K Google TV Review (2026) – Affordable Smart TV with Crisp 4K Entertainment

A 55-inch 4K Google TV offering sharp visuals, smart features, and excellent value for movies, streaming, and everyday entertainment.

Features

  • 55-inch (138cm) 4K Ultra HD LED Display
  • Google TV Smart Platform
  • Dolby Vision Support
  • HDR10 Support
  • Dolby Audio
  • DTS:X Audio
  • Google Assistant Built-in
  • Chromecast Built-in
  • Bezel-Less Design
  • Quad-Core Processor
  • Dual-Band Wi-Fi
  • Bluetooth Connectivity
  • Multiple HDMI and USB Ports

Pros

  • Sharp 4K resolution delivers detailed and clear visuals
  • Google TV provides a smooth and intuitive smart experience
  • Dolby Vision enhances supported HDR content
  • Chromecast built-in makes screen casting effortless
  • Google Assistant enables convenient voice control
  • Slim bezel-less design offers an immersive viewing experience
  • Good connectivity options for gaming consoles and streaming devices
  • Excellent value for a 55-inch 4K smart TV

Cons

  • Standard LED panel cannot match the contrast of QLED or Mini LED TVs
  • Built-in speakers are sufficient but benefit from a soundbar
  • 60Hz refresh rate is not ideal for competitive gaming
  • Brightness is average for HDR content
  • Viewing angles are decent but narrower than OLED displays

Verdict

The Xiaomi X 55-inch 4K Google TV is a well-rounded option for buyers seeking a large-screen smart TV at an affordable price. Its 4K Ultra HD display delivers crisp visuals for movies, sports, and everyday viewing, while Dolby Vision support improves contrast and color in compatible HDR content.

Google TV is one of its biggest strengths, offering a clean interface, personalized recommendations, voice control through Google Assistant, and access to thousands of apps via the Google Play Store. Chromecast built-in also makes it easy to stream content directly from compatible smartphones, tablets, and laptops.

Although it doesn't offer the deeper contrast of QLED or Mini LED televisions, it delivers solid overall picture quality and a reliable smart TV experience. For families looking for a feature-rich 55-inch television without stretching their budget, the Xiaomi X 55-inch 4K Google TV is an excellent value-for-money choice.
